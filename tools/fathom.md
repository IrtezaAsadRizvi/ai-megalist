# Fathom

Fathom is the meeting bot I keep in my back pocket for when Granola can't help — multi room calls, shared phone bridges, anything where local microphone capture isn't enough. The free tier is preposterously generous (unlimited recording, unlimited transcription, no time cap) which is why it's the default for individuals who don't want to pay for a meetings tool.

## What it actually is

A meeting note taker that joins your calls as a participant (the "bot in the call" model). It records, transcribes, summarises, extracts action items, and pushes notes to your CRM, Notion, Slack, or wherever else you wire it. Works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams.

## Setup

1. Go to [fathom.video](https://fathom.video), sign up with Google or Microsoft.
2. Grant calendar access. Fathom auto joins meetings on your calendar (you can toggle this).
3. Install the desktop app (optional) or the Chrome extension.
4. (Optional) Connect integrations: Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Linear.
5. Free tier is unlimited recording. Team tier ($24/user/mo) adds shared workspaces, custom playlists, and CRM auto sync.

## How I use it day to day

* **Customer calls.** Fathom joins, records, transcribes, summarises into a structured note (highlights, decisions, action items). Pushed to HubSpot, linked to the contact.
* **External meetings I'd otherwise forget the details of.** Free, automatic, searchable. The peace of mind alone is worth the (zero) price.
* **Highlights.** During a call, click the Fathom button to mark a moment. The clip is saved with a shareable link, useful for "send me the part where they said X."
* **Action items.** Fathom extracts them automatically; I review and assign.
* **AI ask.** "What did the customer say about pricing across all our calls in March?" Searches across your meeting history.

## Gotchas

* The bot joins as a visible participant ("Fathom Notetaker"). Some attendees object; check your norms.
* Auto join can be aggressive — disable for sensitive 1:1s or it'll show up uninvited.
* Privacy: recordings live on Fathom's cloud. Read the data residency docs if your industry cares.
* The summary quality is good for medium length calls (~30 minutes) and patchier on long sprawling ones.
* Free tier shares meeting data only with you; Team tier shares with your team. Set defaults thoughtfully.
