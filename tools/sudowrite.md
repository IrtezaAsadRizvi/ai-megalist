# Sudowrite

Sudowrite is the AI writing tool fiction writers grudgingly admit they've been using. The general chatbots can write a passable scene; Sudowrite was built around the actual workflow of fiction — rewrites that respect voice, characters with persistent profiles, story bibles, brainstorm tools that don't break flow. Reviewed by The New Yorker, NYT, The Verge as the leader in its category, deservedly.

## What it actually is

A web app for fiction writing. Major features:
* **Story Engine** — turn a synopsis into a draft, scene by scene.
* **Canvas** — long form editor with AI commands inline.
* **Story Bible** — characters, places, plot, lore tracked persistently.
* **Brainstorm** — generate plot, character, world ideas at user level granularity.
* **Rewrite** — adjust prose intensity, length, tone while preserving voice.
* **Visualise** — generate images for scenes or characters.

## Setup

1. Go to [sudowrite.com](https://www.sudowrite.com), sign up.
2. Free trial: 10K words.
3. Pricing: Hobby $19/mo (225K words), Professional $29/mo (1M words), Max $129/mo (3M words).
4. Create a project. Add Story Bible content as you go (characters, world, plot beats).
5. Drop into Canvas to write; use the toolbar for AI commands (Continue, Rewrite, Brainstorm, etc.).

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I'm not a fiction writer. I tested Sudowrite by writing a short story (~5000 words) over a few sessions to evaluate.
* **Continue.** Stuck mid scene; click Continue; Sudowrite produces the next paragraph in your voice. The "in your voice" part is genuinely better than a generic LLM prompt.
* **Story Bible** keeps characters consistent. Drop a character description in once; every AI generation references it.
* **Rewrite for intensity.** "Rewrite this paragraph more visceral" or "more restrained." The output is targeted at the dial you adjusted, unlike "rewrite this" prompts in ChatGPT that drift.
* **Brainstorm for unsticking.** Generate 10 plot turns; pick the one that fits; build from there.
* **Story Engine for first draft scaffold.** A synopsis becomes a chapter outline becomes draft scenes. Faster than starting from blank pages; the output is meant to be heavily revised.

## Gotchas

* AI generated fiction has the "AI ish" tells (over described feelings, generic descriptors). Revise heavily; treat output as a draft, not a final.
* Word usage is generous; hobby tier (225K words/mo) is enough for most writers.
* Story Bible needs maintenance. Out of date bible content produces inconsistent characters.
* The interface has accumulated features over years. Plan an hour of onboarding to feel competent.
* Some publishers require disclosure of AI assistance. Know your contract.
