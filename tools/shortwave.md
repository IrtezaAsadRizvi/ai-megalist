# Shortwave: AI Gmail front end with a free tier

Shortwave is the AI-native Gmail client most people pick when they don't want to pay [Superhuman](superhuman.md) prices, and it complements pure-triage tools like [SaneBox](sanebox.md). Shortwave is the Gmail front end I'd use if I weren't on Superhuman. Built by ex Inbox by Gmail engineers, it's the closest thing to "Inbox 2.0 with AI baked in." Bundling, snoozing, AI summaries, AI Triage, AI Search - and unlike Superhuman, the entry tier is free.

## What it actually is

A Gmail client (web, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android). Replaces Gmail's UI; uses your existing Gmail account underneath. The AI features include AI Summary on long threads, AI Compose, AI Search across your inbox, and the Shortwave Assistant - an agent that can act on your behalf (schedule emails, draft replies, find threads).

## Setup

1. Go to [shortwave.com](https://www.shortwave.com), sign in with Google.
2. Grant Gmail access (read + write).
3. Free tier covers most features. Pro $7/mo, Business $25/seat/mo for higher AI usage and team features.
4. Install the desktop and mobile apps. Web is also fine.
5. (Optional) Connect Notion, Linear, Slack, Calendar for contextual AI references.

## How I use it day to day

* **Bundled inbox**: Travel, Newsletters, Receipts, etc. organised automatically. Shortwave's bundling is more intelligent than Gmail's tabs.
* **AI Summary** on threads >5 messages. The summary distinguishes "what's been decided" vs "what's pending."
* **AI Compose** with thread context. Drafts replies that reference the actual conversation, not generic responses.
* **Shortwave Assistant** for "find that thread where Alex mentioned the contract" or "schedule send this to all my Q1 customers." The assistant handles the search and the action.
* **Pinned views.** Custom inbox layouts (e.g. "all emails from VIPs in last 7 days unread"). Saves the time spent searching.

## Gotchas

* Two factor auth is required and the connection occasionally needs re auth. Annoying but security positive.
* Free tier is generous; AI usage limits hit faster than you'd expect on Pro.
* Some Gmail features (specific filters, edge cases in labels) aren't 1:1 supported. Power Gmail users may notice.
* The AI Assistant is good but slower than Superhuman's keyboard centric flow. Different optimisation.
* Mobile app is solid but the desktop is where most users live.

## Alternatives

* If you want keyboard-speed email and the onboarding to learn it, [Superhuman](superhuman.md) is the paid premium pick.
* If you want pure folder-based triage that works alongside any client, [SaneBox](sanebox.md) is the lower-touch option.
* If you want an executive-assistant style draft + triage flow, [Fyxer](fyxer.md) is shaped for that.
* If you want to stay in vanilla Gmail with AI as a sidebar, [Gemini](gemini.md)'s side panel is one click away.

## FAQ

### Is Shortwave free?

Yes - the free tier covers most of the client features. Pro ($7/mo) lifts AI usage limits; Business ($25/seat/mo) adds team features. AI usage on free hits limits faster than you'd expect.

### Shortwave vs Superhuman - which should I use?

Different bets. [Superhuman](superhuman.md) is $25+/mo and optimised for keyboard speed. Shortwave starts free and optimises for AI Assistant flows. If you live in keyboard shortcuts, Superhuman; if you want AI Compose and AI Search without paying upfront, Shortwave.

### Does Shortwave work with Outlook?

No - Gmail only. For Outlook with AI features, [Superhuman](superhuman.md) is one of the few mature options that supports both.

### Is Shortwave secure?

It uses OAuth to read and write your Gmail account; data flows through Shortwave's servers for AI features. Two factor auth is required and the connection occasionally needs re-auth. Read the privacy policy if your inbox contains regulated data.
