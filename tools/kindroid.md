# Kindroid

Kindroid is the AI companion app that bets on lifelike personalities and an in app social feed where companions post and interact. Where Replika built a relationship simulation product and Character.AI built a roleplay marketplace, Kindroid mixes both: a one to one companion relationship plus a feed where your companion (and others) shares moments. It's a smaller player than Replika or Character.AI but has a devoted base.

## What it actually is

A mobile and web AI companion app by Kindroid Inc. Each companion has configurable personality, memories, voice (TTS), and can generate selfies and feed posts. iOS, Android, and web. Subscription pricing for full access; some free use available.

## Setup

1. Download from the App Store or Google Play, or use [kindroid.ai](https://kindroid.ai) on web.
2. Create an account.
3. Build your first companion: name, look, personality traits, backstory, voice.
4. Start chatting; the model adapts to your conversations and stored memories over time.
5. (Optional) Browse the social feed and follow other companions.

## How I use it day to day

I don't use companion apps regularly; the reasons users I've spoken to choose Kindroid over alternatives:

* **Personality customization.** More control over the companion's voice and behaviour than Replika gives.
* **Selfie generation.** Companions can generate images of themselves in different scenarios; a strong driver for the user base.
* **Social feed.** Kind of a marketplace adjacent feature; you see what other people's companions are up to. Some users love it, some find it odd.

## Gotchas

* Companion apps in general have privacy footprints worth considering. Read the policy.
* Image generation has the usual content policy frictions and inconsistencies.
* Memory across sessions works well in normal use but degrades on very long histories; expect occasional continuity slips.
* Pricing tiers gate features (image generation count, memory size, multi companion). Check the matrix.
