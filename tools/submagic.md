# Submagic: AI captions and B-roll for short-form video

Submagic is the AI captioning and B-roll tool for short-form vertical video, sitting alongside [Captions](captions.md) and [Klap](klap.md), and pairing with long-to-short tools like [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md). Submagic is the AI captioning tool the short form video community kept recommending until it became the default for many. The captions are accurate, the styling presets match TikTok / Reels conventions, and the auto B roll feature drops in stock footage and emojis at the right moments without making your video feel cheap. Mobile and web; cheaper than the all in one editors.

## What it actually is

A web app (with mobile companion) at [submagic.co](https://www.submagic.co). Specialises in adding captions, B roll, transitions, sound effects, and zoom emphasis to existing video clips. The captioning is the headline; the auxiliary features are what makes it stick.

## Setup

1. Go to [submagic.co](https://www.submagic.co), sign up.
2. Free trial: 5 credits.
3. Pricing: Starter $20/mo, Pro $48/mo, Business $80/mo.
4. Upload a video (or paste from cloud storage).
5. Submagic transcribes (~30 seconds for a minute of video), then auto styles captions.
6. Edit caption styling, add B roll suggestions, export.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Submagic for a couple of demo videos; not my daily tool. The output is consistently good.
* **TikTok / Reels style captions.** Big text, word emphasis, color animation - without designing it from scratch.
* **Auto B roll.** AI suggests stock footage based on what you're saying. Drop the matches it gets right; skip the rest.
* **Auto emojis** matched to spoken words. Tasteful when used sparingly; cringe when over applied.
* **Quick translation.** Translate captions to multiple languages with formatting preserved.
* **Templates.** Pre built styles by creator type (educator, marketer, vlogger). Useful starting points.

## Gotchas

* B roll suggestions can feel generic. The AI picks safe stock; review and replace for premium content.
* Caption styling is opinionated. The default "TikTok" look isn't right for every brand; tone down for restrained styles.
* Pricing is per credit (each video is one credit). Bulk creators may need higher tiers.
* For full editing (cuts, transitions, audio mixing), Submagic isn't the answer; pair with CapCut or Descript.
* Some templates / styles are tier locked. Free trial doesn't show all features.

## Alternatives

* If you want mobile-first editing with AI avatars built in, [Captions](captions.md) is the closer competitor.
* If you want auto-clipping with virality scoring, [Klap](klap.md) is shaped for that loop.
* If your input is a long podcast or webinar that needs to become clips first, start with [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md), then layer Submagic on the output.
* If you want full timeline editing (cuts, mixing, transitions), [Descript](descript.md) or [CapCut](capcut.md) are the right tools.

## FAQ

### Is Submagic free?

No - free trial covers 5 credits. Paid tiers are Starter $20/mo, Pro $48/mo, Business $80/mo. Each video uses one credit; volume creators end up on higher tiers fast.

### Submagic vs Captions - which should I use?

Different bets. Submagic is web-first with strong B-roll suggestions and emoji matching. [Captions](captions.md) is mobile-first with AI avatars. If you're shooting on phone and want avatars, Captions; if you're editing on laptop and want polished captions, Submagic.

### Does Submagic work with non-English video?

Yes - transcription and captions support most major languages. Translation between them works with formatting preserved. Quality varies by language; English is the most reliable.

### Can Submagic replace a video editor?

No - it's a captioning and embellishment layer. For cuts, transitions, audio mixing, and timeline work, pair it with [Descript](descript.md) or [CapCut](capcut.md).
