# Otter

Otter has been doing AI meeting transcription longer than most of the entrants in this category, and it shows in the polish and the breadth of features. Where Granola is "bot free, polished after," Otter is "bot in the call, transcribing in real time, searchable forever." Both are valid; Otter is the right choice when you want every word captured and indexed.

## What it actually is

A web + mobile + desktop app for live transcription, meeting notes, and conversation intelligence. Connects to Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and works as a standalone recorder. Features include AI generated summaries, action item extraction, speaker identification, custom vocabulary, and a chat panel ("Otter Chat") that answers questions about your meeting library.

## Setup

1. Go to [otter.ai](https://otter.ai), sign up with Google or Microsoft.
2. Free tier: 300 minutes of transcription/month, max 30 minutes per recording.
3. Pricing: Pro $16.99/mo (1200 min), Business $30/seat/mo (6000 min + admin features), Enterprise.
4. Connect calendar; Otter offers to join meetings on the calendar (toggle per meeting).
5. (Optional) Mobile app for in person meeting recording.
6. (Optional) Otter for Slack — transcripts auto post.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I lean toward Granola for meeting notes; I keep Otter around for cases where I need full searchable transcripts.
* **Live transcription** for accessibility. Useful when joining a meeting where a participant benefits from real time text.
* **In person meetings.** Mobile app records and transcribes a coffee chat or workshop. Speaker labels are decent.
* **Otter Chat for archives.** "What did I say to Acme Corp about pricing last quarter?" Searches across all meetings, returns cited snippets.
* **Action items.** Otter pulls them out automatically; I review and assign.
* **Custom vocabulary.** Add company specific names, acronyms, jargon under Settings → Vocabulary. Improves accuracy noticeably for specialised content.

## Gotchas

* Otter joins meetings as a visible bot ("Otter.ai Notetaker"). Some attendees object; Otter respects do not record requests.
* Transcription is good but not perfect; speaker labels can drift in long calls with similar voices.
* Free tier is enough to evaluate; for daily use the Pro tier is the floor.
* The summary quality depends on meeting structure. Long sprawling discussions produce less useful summaries.
* Privacy: recordings live on Otter's cloud. Read the data residency docs before standardising for regulated work.
