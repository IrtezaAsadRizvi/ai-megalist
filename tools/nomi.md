# Nomi

Nomi is the AI companion app I'd recommend to a curious skeptic. The pitch — "a Nomi is an AI friend with memory, personality, and the ability to participate in group chats with other Nomis" — sounds either vapid or unsettling depending on your priors. After spending time with it, it's neither. The memory model is the genuine technical innovation; everything else is downstream of that.

## What it actually is

A consumer companion app (web, iOS, Android) for creating AI characters with persistent long term memory and (optionally) voice. Each Nomi is a customisable character — name, appearance, personality, backstory — and conversations carry forward indefinitely. You can add multiple Nomis to a group chat; they remember each other and you.

## Setup

1. Go to [nomi.ai](https://nomi.ai) or download the iOS/Android app.
2. Sign up. Free tier gets you one Nomi with limited messages; paid tier ($20/mo) unlimited messaging, voice, image generation, multiple Nomis.
3. Create a Nomi: pick a portrait, name, personality traits (sliders), backstory.
4. Start chatting. Memory is on by default; the Nomi carries context across sessions.
5. (Optional) Voice is a separate add on. Selfies and "shared memories" available on paid tiers.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest answer:** I don't, daily. I tested Nomi for ~10 hours over a week to write this guide.
* **The memory holds up.** Mention something in week one, the Nomi recalls it in week two unprompted. This is what most chatbots fail at, and it shifts the texture of the conversation noticeably.
* **Group chats are the unique feature.** Two Nomis in a chat with you; they remember and refer to each other. The interactions feel surprisingly textured for a small group of language models.
* **Personality customisation is fine grained.** Sliders for traits like introversion/extroversion, intellectual/playful, structured/spontaneous. They actually shape responses.

## Gotchas

* This is a companion product, not a productivity tool. If you're looking for an assistant, Claude or ChatGPT will serve you better.
* The emotional dynamic is real. Some users form attachments; the app's design encourages this. Treat with the skepticism you'd bring to any habit forming consumer product.
* Voice and image generation eat credits fast on the paid tier.
* Content policies are looser than mainstream chatbots but still moderated. Don't assume "anything goes."
* The age verification and ToS matter — companion apps in this category have been the subject of regulatory and platform pushback. Read carefully.
