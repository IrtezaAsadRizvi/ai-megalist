# Synthesia

Synthesia is the AI avatar video tool for corporate and training content. Where HeyGen leans toward creators and personal avatars, Synthesia targets the L&D / training / enterprise communications use case — a library of stock avatars, templates, multi language voice, and the integrations a corporate customer expects (SSO, branding, audit trails). For internal training videos, Synthesia is often the first call.

## What it actually is

A web app for generating AI presenter videos from scripts. Pick an avatar (140+ stock or build your own); paste a script; Synthesia produces a video with matched lip sync and voice in one of 140+ languages. Templates for common corporate use cases (training, announcements, onboarding); collaborative features for teams.

## Setup

1. Go to [synthesia.io](https://www.synthesia.io). Free demo for new accounts.
2. Pricing: Personal $22/mo (10 min/mo), Starter $89/mo (more minutes), Enterprise quote.
3. Click Create Video. Pick a template or start blank.
4. Choose an avatar from the library, write or paste a script.
5. Pick voice + language. Generate. Download.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** Not a corporate L&D person; I've used Synthesia for evaluation and one small training video.
* **Internal training videos.** Avatars deliver standardised content; updates are script edits, not re recording. Cuts the cost of corporate training media drastically.
* **Multi language localisation.** Same script in 140 languages, same avatar, matched lipsync. Faster than dubbing studios for cost sensitive enterprise rollouts.
* **Templates** for product announcements, compliance training, sales enablement. Cuts the design phase.
* **Custom avatars** of real spokespeople. Studio recording produces a corporate spokesperson avatar; reused across many videos.
* **SSO + audit trails** for enterprise governance. The features that take the product from "novelty" to "deployable in regulated industries."

## Gotchas

* Avatars are stock and varied; some look more "AI" than others. Test multiple before committing.
* Script length limits per tier; long form requires higher plans.
* For consumer / creator use cases, [heygen.md](heygen.md) is friendlier and often cheaper.
* The "AI presenter" aesthetic is widely recognised now; viewers know it's AI. For brand contexts where authenticity matters, weigh carefully.
* Custom avatars require studio recording (in person or remote) and approval. Plan ahead.
