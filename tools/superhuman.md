# Superhuman

Superhuman is the email client that taught me email is a keyboard sport. Before Superhuman I clicked through Gmail like a tourist. After Superhuman I have muscle memory for ten shortcuts I use a hundred times a day. The AI features are the new layer; the keyboard speed is the foundation.

## What it actually is

A premium email client (web, macOS, iOS, Android) on top of Gmail or Outlook. The AI features include AI Compose (drafts in your voice), AI Triage (sorts and summarises your inbox), AI Reply (suggests responses), and AI Search (natural language across all email). The non AI features — keyboard shortcuts, snippets, scheduled send, inbox split — are also genuinely better than anything else in this category.

## Setup

1. Go to [superhuman.com](https://superhuman.com), sign up.
2. Pricing: Starter $25/mo, Business $40/mo. No free tier.
3. Connect your Gmail or Outlook (read/write access required).
4. Onboarding includes a real human walkthrough of keyboard shortcuts (a 20 minute Zoom). Take it; the muscle memory shift is the actual product.
5. Set up Snippets, Splits (separate views for important vs newsletters vs notifications), and AI features under Settings.

## How I use it day to day

* **Cmd+K for everything.** Move, archive, label, snooze — all keyboard. I haven't touched my mouse for email in years.
* **Inbox Split.** Important on top, news/notifications below the fold. Cuts the cognitive load of scrolling past mailing lists.
* **AI Triage.** Newer feature; auto categorises and summarises long threads. The summaries are good enough that I read them instead of the full thread for ~30% of incoming.
* **AI Compose / Reply.** "Reply yes, propose Tuesday, formal tone." Superhuman drafts; I edit. Saves the most time on routine responses.
* **Snippets** for canned text. `;intro` expands to my standard intro paragraph. Saves 2 minutes per first email.
* **Snooze.** The thread comes back at the time I picked. The "remind me if no reply" feature is the close second.

## Gotchas

* Pricing is steep. The argument is "saves you 4+ hours a week"; verify on your own usage before committing.
* The keyboard heavy design is great if you live in email and miserable if you don't. Power users only.
* AI features are gated by the model used. They've been improving fast; check the changelog.
* It's still a Gmail/Outlook front end. If your IT requires the native client, Superhuman may not be approved.
* No native scheduling, no native task list. Pair with [motion.md](motion.md) or similar.
