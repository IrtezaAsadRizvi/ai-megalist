# Writer

Writer is the enterprise grade content platform that takes brand governance seriously. While Jasper and Copy.ai chase mid market marketers, Writer goes after Fortune 500 communications and brand teams with a full stack: their own model family (Palmyra), a brand voice engine, a knowledge graph that grounds outputs in approved content, and on prem deployment options. It's a different kind of product from a chat assistant; it's infrastructure.

## What it actually is

An enterprise generative AI platform built around content governance. Includes Writer's own Palmyra LLM family, a brand voice and style engine, an enterprise knowledge graph (Writer Knowledge Graph), AI Studio for building no code AI apps, and integrations across the Microsoft and Google productivity stacks. Subscription pricing; enterprise contracts only at scale.

## Setup

1. Request a demo at [writer.com](https://writer.com). The product isn't really self serve at the high tier; sales involvement is expected for the full feature set.
2. There's also a Team plan with a free trial for smaller orgs. Sign up via the website.
3. Define your brand: voice attributes, terminology, style rules, banned words.
4. Connect knowledge sources (Confluence, SharePoint, etc.) so AI outputs are grounded in approved content.
5. Roll out via the browser extension, the Word and Docs add ins, or the API.

## How I use it day to day

I don't use Writer myself; I've seen it used inside larger orgs and the value proposition is consistent.

* **Compliance and brand at scale.** The reason large orgs pick Writer over ChatGPT Enterprise is the governance layer: outputs are checked against brand rules in real time.
* **Knowledge grounding.** AI outputs cite internal sources, not random web pages. This is the killer feature for legal and regulated industries.
* **AI Studio for internal apps.** Build a no code AI assistant for a specific function (sales enablement, HR FAQs) without standing up a custom RAG stack.

## Gotchas

* Pricing is enterprise. If you're a solo user or small team, this is overkill; use Jasper or just Claude.
* The Palmyra models lag the absolute frontier on raw capability. The trade is governance and control, not peak performance.
* On prem and VPC deployment exist but require negotiation; not a self serve flow.
* The product surface is broad; some customers use only the brand voice engine, others go all in on AI Studio. Plan your rollout.
