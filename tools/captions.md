# Captions: mobile-first AI video editor with eye-contact correction

Captions sits in the video editing cluster alongside [CapCut](capcut.md), [Descript](descript.md), and [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md), but uniquely mobile-first and built around the talking-head short-form workflow. Captions (the iOS / Android app) is the mobile first AI video editor for short form creators. Auto captions, auto B roll, AI Eye Contact (correct gaze when reading from notes), AI Avatars, voice clone, scene reframing - all on phone, all reasonably fast. For TikTok / Reels / Shorts creators, it's the fastest path from "raw selfie video" to "publish ready short."

## What it actually is

A mobile app from Captions LLC (also a smaller web app). Specialises in talking head video for vertical short form. Features include:
* Auto captions with extensive styling.
* AI Eye Contact - corrects your gaze even when you read from notes off camera.
* AI Studio - generate full videos from a script, with avatars and voice clones.
* AI Edit - auto trim filler, auto cut between angles, auto add B roll.
* Voice clone for translation / dubbing.

## Setup

1. Download from the App Store / Google Play. Web app at [captions.ai](https://www.captions.ai).
2. Free tier: limited features, exports watermarked.
3. Pricing: Pro $9.99/mo (mobile), Captions Studio $29/mo (more AI features), Business $59/mo.
4. Record in app or import an existing clip. Captions transcribes in seconds.
5. Edit captions, apply Eye Contact / B roll / etc., export.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Captions for short demo videos; not a daily creator.
* **AI Eye Contact** is the killer feature. Read from a teleprompter; the app corrects your eyes to look straight at the camera. Subtle and effective.
* **Auto captions** with TikTok style emphasis and word level animation. Faster than CapCut for caption styling.
* **Auto trim filler.** Remove "ums," long pauses, false starts in a single tap. Cleaner version of the same feature in Descript.
* **Voice clone for translation.** Record in English; export in Spanish with your own voice. Comparable to HeyGen's translation.
* **AI Studio for fully generated videos.** Type a script, pick an avatar (or use a clone of yourself), get a full video. Lower fidelity than HeyGen Studio Avatars but faster.

## Gotchas

* Mobile first. Web app is improving but the polish is on iOS / Android.
* Eye Contact is uncanny in close ups; subtle in mid shots. Test on your typical framing.
* Voice clone needs a quiet recording for the training step. Phone microphones in noisy environments give weak clones.
* Pricing is affordable but features are tier locked; advanced AI lives on Captions Studio.
* For long form (>5 minutes), Captions is awkward. CapCut or Descript fit better.

## Alternatives

* If you want a free desktop and mobile editor with strong auto-captions, [CapCut](capcut.md) is the broader comparator.

## FAQ

### Is Captions free?

Free tier exports are watermarked and feature-limited. Pro is $9.99/mo on mobile; Captions Studio $29/mo for AI features; Business $59/mo. Pricing scales with how much AI you actually use.

### What is AI Eye Contact?

The killer feature - it corrects your gaze even when you read from notes off camera. Uncanny in close-ups, subtle in mid-shots. Test on your typical framing before committing.

### Captions vs CapCut - which one?

Different bets. [CapCut](capcut.md) is a free general-purpose editor with great auto-captions; Captions is more specialised for talking-head selfie video with Eye Contact, AI Avatars, and AI Studio. For general short-form, CapCut. For talking-head-specific tooling, Captions.

### Does Captions clone voices?

Yes - record clean audio in a quiet room (phone microphones in noisy environments give weak clones). Voice clone supports translation and dubbing. Quality is below [ElevenLabs](elevenlabs.md) but built into the editing flow.

### Can Captions handle long-form video?

Awkwardly. Above ~5 minutes, [CapCut](capcut.md) or [Descript](descript.md) fit better. Captions' sweet spot is 30-second to 3-minute talking-head shorts.
