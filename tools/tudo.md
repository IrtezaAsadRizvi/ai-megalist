# Tudo: AI task capture and personal planning

Tudo turns quick thoughts, loose reminders, and daily planning notes into organized tasks and next actions. It is built for people who want a light personal planning app rather than a heavyweight team project-management system.

## What it actually is

An iOS-first AI task capture app. You can use it to capture tasks quickly, structure them into clearer action items, and keep personal planning moving without spending too much time organizing the planner itself.

## Best for

* Capturing tasks before they disappear from working memory.
* Turning rough notes into specific next actions.
* Lightweight personal planning and day-to-day organization.
