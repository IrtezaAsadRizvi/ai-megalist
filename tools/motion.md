# Motion

Motion is the calendar app that schedules itself. You add tasks with priorities and durations; Motion picks where they go on your calendar, defends the time, and reshuffles when something changes. The first week feels weird (the AI moved my meetings); the second week, you realise you've been planning your day wrong for years.

## What it actually is

A web + iOS/Android app combining calendar, tasks, and projects, with an AI scheduler that auto books focus time. Connects to Google Calendar, iCal, and Outlook. There's a project management layer (Motion replaces Asana / ClickUp for some teams) but the calendar is the headline.

## Setup

1. Go to [usemotion.com](https://www.usemotion.com), sign up.
2. Pricing: Pro $19/mo (annual), $34/mo (monthly). No free tier — there's a free trial.
3. Connect your calendar(s). Motion treats your existing meetings as fixed and schedules tasks around them.
4. Set work hours (when Motion is allowed to schedule), set priorities (urgent vs medium), add tasks with estimated durations.
5. Motion produces a daily plan. Adjust the plan or trust it.

About 15 minutes to set up; the value compounds over weeks.

## How I use it day to day

* **Tasks with deadlines and durations.** "Write the Q2 doc, 4 hours, due Friday." Motion schedules it as a 2 hour block on Tuesday and a 2 hour block on Wednesday. If a meeting moves, Motion reflows.
* **Auto scheduling for recurring routines.** Daily tasks (morning review, email triage) get fixed slots; flexible tasks fill the gaps.
* **Calendar as the source of truth.** I plan in Motion, but the actual calendar (Google) shows my colleagues blocks they can't book. The defense of focus time is the win.
* **Mobile during meetings.** Quick capture from phone; Motion files into the right project.
* **The Motion Booking link.** Clients pick from genuinely available time, not from "what looks free." Better than Calendly's dumber heuristics.

## Gotchas

* Habit cost is real for the first week. Trust the scheduler; the second week feels different.
* Conflicts between Motion and other reminder apps are annoying. Pick one source of truth.
* Calendar mods from outside Motion (someone moves your meeting in Google) sometimes confuse the scheduler. Refresh.
* The project management features are decent but if your team uses Linear / Jira, Motion's PM layer feels like overhead. Use it for personal scheduling and ignore the team features.
* Pricing is high vs comparable tools. The argument is that better focus time is worth it; verify on your own week before paying.
