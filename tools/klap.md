# Klap

Klap turns long videos into short ones, scores them by likely virality, and gives you ten clips from a single hour of podcast you upload. It's one of half a dozen products in this category; the differentiator is that Klap leans hard into the "virality scoring" framing, which both helps you pick clips faster and is a kind of marketing flourish.

## What it actually is

A web app that converts long form video (podcasts, lectures, interviews) into short vertical clips formatted for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Auto adds captions, picks the speaker focused crop, applies basic edits, and ranks clips by predicted engagement. Subscription pricing.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [klap.app](https://klap.app). Free trial; usage gated by clip count.
2. Upload a video file or paste a YouTube URL.
3. Wait for processing (a few minutes for a one hour video).
4. Klap returns a list of suggested clips with a virality score, captions, and a vertical crop preview.
5. Edit any clip in the built in editor; export to MP4 or post directly via the integrations.

## How I use it day to day

I don't post short form content myself, but I've used Klap a few times to clip podcasts I've been on, and the workflow is fast:

* **Bulk first pass.** Upload, get ten clips, look at them. Most are mediocre, two or three are good. The hit rate is in line with what creators describe.
* **Pick on virality score selectively.** The score is directionally useful but not a god. Sometimes the highest scored clip is wrong for the actual audience; sometimes the bottom clip is the gem.
* **Light edit before posting.** Captions sometimes mistranscribe technical terms. Always proofread.

## Gotchas

* Auto captions accuracy varies; edit before posting if your content has jargon.
* The vertical crop sometimes loses important visual context (slides, charts). Watch the previews.
* Virality score is a heuristic, not a guarantee. Treat as a tie breaker, not a sort key.
* Pricing is per minute of input video; long uploads burn credits fast.
