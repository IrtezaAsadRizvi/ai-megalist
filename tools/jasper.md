# Jasper

Jasper is the marketing AI platform that figured out enterprises early. Where most AI writing tools are "GPT with a nicer UI," Jasper sells brand voice, templated workflows, campaign management, and a compliance story. It's the tool I recommend when someone tells me "we need an AI that respects our brand guidelines and doesn't go rogue in customer copy."

## What it actually is

A marketing focused AI platform built around brand voice training, content templates, and team collaboration. Sits on top of the major frontier models with proprietary tuning and workflow tooling. Web app plus Chrome extension plus integrations into common marketing stacks (HubSpot, Google Docs, etc.). Subscription pricing skews business and enterprise.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [jasper.ai](https://www.jasper.ai). Free trial; paid plans start at the Creator tier.
2. Configure your Brand Voice: paste sample copy, Jasper extracts tone and style attributes you can apply to any future generation.
3. Build a Brand Knowledge base: facts, product details, audience info that should influence every output.
4. (Optional) Add team members; assign roles and approval workflows.
5. (Optional) Install the Chrome extension to use Jasper inside Gmail, Docs, LinkedIn.

## How I use it day to day

* **When the brief is "make this sound like the brand."** Jasper's voice tuning is the most rigorous I've used. Outputs are recognisably on brand without manual rewrites.
* **Campaign generation.** Templates for ad copy, email sequences, landing pages. Output is solid first draft material; humans still need to ship the final.
* **For non technical marketers.** The UI is friendlier than dropping people directly into ChatGPT or Claude. Less fear of breaking something.

For my own writing I don't use Jasper; I'd rather be in a chat with Claude. Jasper earns its place when there's a team and a brand to protect.

## Gotchas

* Pricing is steep for solo users. Sustained ROI shows up at team scale.
* The brand voice training is good but not magical. Garbage in, garbage out.
* Underlying models update; sometimes a workflow that worked great last quarter regresses. Keep eval prompts handy.
* The Chrome extension can be flaky on certain sites, especially LinkedIn after redesigns.
