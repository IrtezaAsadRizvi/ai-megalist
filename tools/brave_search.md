# Brave Search: privacy-first AI search with an independent index

Brave Search sits in the AI search cluster alongside [Perplexity](perplexity.md), [Kagi](kagi.md), and [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com) - the privacy-first option with its own index and a free tier. Brave Search is the independent search engine with its own index. The AI features (Brave Leo for in browser chat, AI Answers in search results, Brave Summarizer) are layered on top. Where Google AI Overviews and Bing Copilot are reactions, Brave's AI was built into a privacy first product from the start. For users who care about not being tracked while searching, Brave is the credible choice.

## What it actually is

A search engine + AI suite. Components:
* **Brave Search**: independent web search with its own index.
* **AI Answers**: cited summaries above search results (similar to Google's AI Overviews).
* **Brave Leo**: sidebar AI assistant in the Brave browser.
* **Brave Summarizer**: quick summary feature for search queries.

Free; ad supported. There's also a paid Brave Search Premium that turns off ads.

## Setup

1. Use [search.brave.com](https://search.brave.com) directly, or set as default search in any browser.
2. Install the Brave browser ([brave.com](https://brave.com)) for the full Leo experience.
3. Brave Leo: in the browser, click the AI icon on the right. Free tier: limited usage. Premium ($14.99/mo) unlocks more advanced models (Claude, Mistral, Llama).
4. AI Answers appear automatically on Brave Search results when relevant.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I default to Google + Perplexity; I use Brave Search occasionally for the privacy story.
* **As a Google alternative.** The independent index is real; results differ from Google for many queries (sometimes better, sometimes worse).
* **Privacy mode.** Brave Search doesn't track me. For sensitive queries, the no log policy matters.
* **Brave Leo as a free chat sidebar.** Inside the Brave browser, summarise the current page or ask follow up questions. Comparable to Edge Copilot or Comet's sidebar.
* **AI Answers** for quick factual lookups. Cited; concise.
* **Goggles** (custom search ranking rules) is a niche but powerful feature. Define a "goggle" that re ranks results by your preferences (e.g. "no listicle sites").

## Gotchas

* The independent index is real but smaller than Google's. For long tail / niche queries, Google sometimes finds things Brave doesn't.
* AI features are present but less polished than Perplexity's or Comet's. The free tier is enough to evaluate.
* Brave Leo's "smartest" models are Premium gated; the free tier uses lighter models.
* Brave the browser bundles a lot of features (rewards, wallet, BAT crypto). Not everyone wants those - they're toggleable but visible.
* For pure search privacy without AI: DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Kagi (paid).

## Alternatives

## FAQ

### Is Brave Search free?

Yes, ad-supported. There's a paid Brave Search Premium that turns off ads, plus Brave Leo Premium ($14.99/mo) for access to better models in the browser sidebar.

### Brave Search vs Perplexity - which is better?

Different jobs. Brave Search is a real search engine with its own index plus AI summaries; [Perplexity](perplexity.md) is a chat-style research tool. For "find me a page" Brave wins; for "synthesise five sources" Perplexity wins.

### Does Brave Search have its own index?

Yes - that's the differentiator. Most "alt search" engines re-rank Bing or Google. Brave maintains its own crawl, which means results genuinely differ - sometimes better, sometimes worse on long tail queries.

### What is Brave Leo?

The AI sidebar inside the Brave browser - summarise the current page, ask follow-up questions. Free tier uses lighter models; Premium ($14.99/mo) unlocks Claude, Mistral, and Llama variants.

### What are Goggles?

Custom search ranking rules. Define a "goggle" that re-ranks Brave results by your preferences (e.g. "no listicle sites"). Niche but effective for power users.
