# Endel

Endel is the AI generated soundscape app for focus, sleep, relaxation, and exercise. Where Suno generates songs and Mubert generates loops, Endel generates *adaptive ambient music* that responds to time of day, weather, heart rate, and your activity. It's been Apple Design Award winning since 2019; the AI features have deepened steadily.

## What it actually is

A consumer mobile / desktop app and Apple Watch app. Soundscapes are generated continuously, never repeating, parameterised by your context (time, weather, biometrics, activity). The science framing is real — Endel has published research with neuroscience labs on the effects on focus and sleep.

## Setup

1. Download from the App Store / Google Play / web. macOS, Windows, Apple Watch, Apple TV.
2. Free tier: limited soundscapes; access to "Focus" only.
3. Pricing: $49.99/year. Family plan available.
4. Pick a "Mode" — Focus, Relax, Sleep, On the Go, Move.
5. Soundscape starts; runs as long as you want; adapts to context.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Endel intermittently for focus work and sleep.
* **Focus soundscapes** during deep work blocks. Less distracting than music with vocals; less monotonous than white noise.
* **Sleep soundscapes** at night. Endel slows the texture as the night progresses; designed for falling and staying asleep.
* **Apple Watch integration.** Heart rate from the watch shapes the soundscape; calming when stressed, energising when needed.
* **Relax** mode for breaks. Different texture from focus; signals to my brain "different mode."
* **As one of three audio sources I rotate** depending on context — Endel for focus, Spotify for variety, silence sometimes.

## Gotchas

* Subjective whether the AI generated soundscapes work better than fixed playlists. Personal preference.
* No offline mode in older versions; check if relevant.
* Some users find the lack of variety boring; Endel deliberately stays uniform within a session.
* Pricing is annual; the free tier is enough to evaluate but limited.
* For active music with lyrics during workouts: Spotify / Apple Music. Endel is for the "no lyrics, just texture" use case.
