# Speak

Speak is the AI conversation partner for language learners. Where Duolingo Max layers AI on top of a curriculum, Speak is conversation first — the entire experience is "talk to the AI in your target language about something." For learners past beginner level, the unscripted speaking practice is what closes the gap to fluency.

## What it actually is

A mobile app (iOS + Android) and web app from Speak. Built around free form spoken conversation with an AI tutor. Features: AI Tutor (open ended conversation), Roleplays (scenario based practice), Video Lessons, Quick Drills (short focused practice). Initially a Korean only app; expanded to English (for non English speakers), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Mandarin.

## Setup

1. Download from the App Store / Google Play.
2. Free tier: 3 lessons/day.
3. Pricing: Premium $14/mo annual, $99/year. Premium Plus (unlimited AI Tutor) $24/mo annual.
4. Pick target language; Speak assesses your level (CEFR A1 to C1).
5. Daily routine: video lesson → drills → conversation practice with AI Tutor.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Speak for ~6 months on Spanish alongside Duolingo Max.
* **AI Tutor for daily speaking.** 10 minutes of unscripted Spanish a day. The AI asks follow up questions, corrects subtly, and matches my level. Comparable to a (less structured) tutor but available always.
* **Roleplays for situational practice.** Order at a restaurant, ask for directions, negotiate at a market. Better for practical fluency than abstract grammar drills.
* **Speech recognition is the central feature.** Surprisingly forgiving of accents; correctly hears me even on cluttered phone audio.
* **Corrections are tonal not punitive.** The AI doesn't make you feel dumb for mistakes. Lower barrier to actually speaking.
* **Pairs well with structured curricula.** Speak is conversation; Duolingo / textbooks fill in grammar.

## Gotchas

* The model occasionally accepts wrong forms as correct, especially with non standard pronunciation. Don't rely on Speak alone for grammatical precision.
* The conversation can feel repetitive after a while; new topics require steering.
* Speech recognition struggles in noisy environments; quiet space + decent mic improves reliability.
* No human tutor in the loop. For accountability and nuanced feedback, supplement with italki or in person classes.
* Pricing: Premium Plus ($24/mo annual) is real money. Verify on free tier whether it suits your style.
