# CapCut: AI video editor for short-form social

CapCut sits in the video editing cluster alongside [Descript](descript.md), [Captions](captions.md), and [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md), but unique in being TikTok's own editor with deep AI integration on every platform. CapCut is the video editor TikTok built and made everyone else use. The desktop and mobile apps are free for most features, the AI tools (auto captions, background removal, voice cloning, magic edits) are deeply integrated, and the export presets target every social platform. For short form social video, CapCut is the path of least resistance.

## What it actually is

A free video editor by ByteDance (TikTok's parent), available on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and web. AI features include auto captions, auto subtitle, background removal, voice cloning, AI scripts, AI thumbnails, "Magic Tools" for color and stabilisation, and integration with TikTok's effect library.

## Setup

1. Download from [capcut.com](https://www.capcut.com). Free.
2. CapCut Pro tier ($7.99/mo for 200 cloud minutes, more AI features).
3. Sign in (optional for cloud sync; required for some AI features).
4. Import media, drop on timeline, edit. The UI is closer to a consumer video editor than a professional NLE.

## How I use it day to day

* **Auto captions.** Drop a clip in, click captions, get accurate transcription in 5 seconds with hundreds of styling options. The fastest captioning workflow in any editor.
* **Background removal.** One click; works on people in most scenes. For greenscreen replacement on a budget.
* **Auto reframe** from 16:9 to 9:16 with face tracking. CapCut's mobile app does this best of any free tool.
* **Magic Tools.** Auto color, auto enhance, stabilise. Quick fixes that would take 10 minutes in Premiere take 5 seconds.
* **TikTok ready presets.** Aspect ratio, length, audio sync - everything tuned for TikTok / Reels / Shorts.
* **Voice cloning** for translation / voiceovers. Decent quality; not ElevenLabs tier but serviceable.

## Gotchas

* CapCut is owned by ByteDance. In some jurisdictions, especially the US in regulated sectors, this is a concern. Check policies.
* Some AI features in the desktop / web version are paywalled where the mobile equivalents are free. Inconsistent.
* For professional work (broadcast, cinema), CapCut isn't the answer; DaVinci Resolve or Premiere are. CapCut's sweet spot is short form social.
* Cloud sync uploads your media to ByteDance servers. Disable for private content.
* Watermarks on exports were toggled in/out of free tier multiple times. Check current policy.

## Alternatives

* If you want to edit a video by editing its transcript, [Descript](descript.md) is the canonical text-based editor.
* If you want to turn a long podcast into short clips with virality scoring, [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md) is the dedicated tool.
* If you want mobile-first AI captions, eye-contact correction, and AI avatars, [Captions](captions.md) is the comparator.
* If you need professional broadcast-grade editing, DaVinci Resolve or Premiere are still the answer; CapCut is for short-form social.

## FAQ

### Is CapCut free?

Yes, free for most features. CapCut Pro is $7.99/mo for 200 cloud minutes and additional AI features. Watermarks on free-tier exports have been toggled in/out multiple times - check current policy.

### CapCut vs Descript - which one?

Different shapes. CapCut is a timeline editor with great auto-captions and aspect-ratio reframing. [Descript](descript.md) is a transcript-based editor where editing the text edits the video. CapCut for social shorts; Descript for podcasts and long-form.

### Is CapCut safe to use? (ByteDance concerns)

CapCut is owned by ByteDance. In some jurisdictions and regulated sectors (especially the US), this is a real concern. Cloud sync uploads media to ByteDance servers - disable for private content, and check organisational policies.

### What's the best CapCut feature?

Auto-captions with TikTok-style emphasis - fastest captioning workflow in any free editor. The auto-reframe from 16:9 to 9:16 with face tracking is the runner-up.

### Does CapCut have AI voice cloning?

Yes - decent quality, not [ElevenLabs](elevenlabs.md) tier. Serviceable for translation and voiceover work where you don't need broadcast-grade output.
