# Khanmigo

Khanmigo is the AI tutor Khan Academy built and gave away (subsidised) for free to students, teachers, and parents. The pedagogy is the point — Khanmigo is trained not to just give answers but to ask the questions that lead a learner to the answer. For K‑12 work, it's the closest thing to a Socratic method tutor you can give every student.

## What it actually is

An AI assistant integrated into Khan Academy's learning platform. Built on top of GPT‑4o (and successors), with custom prompts that constrain it to a tutoring stance. Available for math, science, humanities, and writing assistance. There are separate experiences for students, teachers, and parents.

## Setup

1. Go to [khanmigo.ai](https://www.khanmigo.ai). Pricing has shifted over time; the parent / individual tier is currently $4/mo, free for teachers in the US.
2. Sign up with a Khan Academy account.
3. Pick role: student, teacher, parent.
4. For students, Khanmigo appears alongside Khan Academy lessons — you ask it questions while working through content.
5. Teachers get planning tools (rubric generation, lesson outlines); parents get progress monitoring.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I don't have a school aged kid; I tested Khanmigo by working through Khan Academy math units and asking for help.
* **The Socratic stance is real.** Ask Khanmigo for the answer; it responds with a guiding question. Frustrating if you want the answer; useful if you want to learn.
* **Teacher tools.** Lesson plans, rubric generation, "explain this concept three ways for different learners." Genuinely good.
* **Math help especially.** Khanmigo recognises common student errors and addresses them by name rather than just correcting.
* **Writing tutor.** Submits drafts; Khanmigo asks "what's your thesis here?" before reviewing the prose. Good for high school essay practice.

## Gotchas

* It will *not* just give answers, even when you ask. This is the design; some users find it limiting.
* The model is gated to school appropriate topics. Off topic questions get redirected.
* The integration is tightest with Khan Academy's own content. If you're learning from a textbook outside of KA, Khanmigo helps less.
* The tutoring style works best for K‑12. For college level work, ChatGPT or Claude's "explain like I'm a graduate student" prompts are often more useful.
* For multilingual learners, English is the strongest experience; other languages vary.
