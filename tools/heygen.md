# HeyGen

HeyGen is the AI avatar tool that crossed the "uncanny valley to actually usable" line first. You upload a 2 minute video of yourself; HeyGen builds a digital twin that can speak any script in any of 175+ languages, with matched lipsync. For corporate explainers, course videos, and multilingual marketing, the productivity gain is genuine.

## What it actually is

A web app for generating talking head videos with AI avatars. Three flavors:
* **Stock avatars** — pre built characters you can put a script in front of.
* **Instant Avatar** — your face + voice, trained from a 2 minute clip.
* **Studio Avatar** — high quality version that requires a 30+ minute studio recording (can be done at home).

Plus translation, lipsync to existing footage, video templates, and an API for programmatic generation.

## Setup

1. Go to [heygen.com](https://www.heygen.com), sign up.
2. Free tier: 1 minute of video/month, watermarked.
3. Pricing: Creator $24/mo, Team $69/seat/mo, Enterprise.
4. Click Create Avatar → Instant Avatar → record 2 minutes of yourself talking to camera.
5. Wait ~10 minutes for the model to train.
6. Type a script, pick a voice (your cloned voice or stock), generate.

## How I use it day to day

* **Multilingual versions of one source.** Record once in English; HeyGen produces French, Spanish, German, Japanese versions with my face and matched lips. Used for product launches.
* **Course videos at scale.** Update a script section, regenerate; no re recording. Course platforms love this because it makes content evergreen.
* **Quick personal explainers.** Send a short "here's what I'm thinking" video to a stakeholder without putting on a clean shirt.
* **Translation of existing video.** Upload an old presentation, get it back in another language. The lipsync isn't perfect on side angles but holds for direct shots.
* **API for templating.** Programmatic generation of personalised videos at scale (e.g. onboarding videos with the customer's name and details).

## Gotchas

* Avatars on close inspection still read as AI. Useful for B roll and explainers; less useful for high stakes brand work.
* Studio Avatars look much better than Instant ones. If you'll use this seriously, do the studio recording.
* Voice cloning quality depends on your recording quality. Quiet room, decent mic, clear enunciation.
* Lipsync on heavily inflected languages (Mandarin, Vietnamese) is improving but not as crisp as on English.
* Some industries (regulated finance, legal) prohibit AI generated likenesses in client communication. Check before deploying.
