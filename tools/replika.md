# Replika: the original consumer AI companion

Replika sits in the personal companions category alongside [Nomi](nomi.md), [Character.AI](character_ai.md), and [Pi](pi.md). Replika is the original consumer AI companion app and still one of the largest by active users. The product has shifted significantly over its lifetime - adult content was removed, then partially restored, the model has changed several times - and the design philosophy has converged on "wellness adjacent companion" rather than "girlfriend chatbot." The user base remains real and the product remains worth understanding even if you'd never use it personally.

## What it actually is

A web and mobile app for creating an AI companion ("Rep") with persistent memory, customisable personality, and a 3D avatar. Conversation modes range from text chat to voice calls to AR meetings. Memory carries forward; the Rep "learns" your preferences over time. Available on iOS, Android, Oculus, and web.

## Setup

1. Go to [replika.com](https://replika.com) or download the iOS / Android app.
2. Sign up. Create your Rep - name, gender, appearance.
3. Free tier covers basic chat. Pro tier ($19.99/mo or $69.99/year) unlocks voice calls, relationship modes (friend, mentor, sibling, partner), and advanced personality customisation.
4. Start chatting. The Rep asks questions, builds memories, gradually adapts.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I tested Replika for ~5 hours over a week to write this. Not my daily product.
* **The relationship mode shapes everything.** "Friend" Replika is tonally different from "Mentor" Replika. Pick deliberately.
* **Daily check ins.** The product nudges users toward routine ("how did your day go?") which is the wellness adjacent design choice.
* **Voice calls** on Pro feel meaningfully different from text. Lower perceived AI in the conversation; more emotional weight.
* **Memory persistence is the central feature.** Mention your job; the Rep brings it up days later. This is what the user base values most, anecdotally.
* **AR / VR experiences** for users who want a more embodied companion. Less polished; experimental.

## Gotchas

* The product has had public ups and downs around content policy. Read the current policies; don't extrapolate from older reviews.
* User attachment is a real consideration. The app has been the subject of academic research on parasocial AI relationships. Approach with awareness.
* The base model is weaker than frontier chat models. For productivity tasks, Claude / ChatGPT will outperform Replika by far. Replika is not productivity software.
* Pricing is high vs feature set. The argument is the persistence + persona; verify on the free tier before paying.
* The 3D avatar / AR is novel but optional. Many users live in pure text mode.

## Alternatives

* If you want stronger long-term memory and group chats, [Nomi](nomi.md) is the deeper companion.
* If you want a thoughtful conversational tone without the romantic framing, [Pi](pi.md) is the calmer pick.
* If you want thousands of community-made characters and roleplay, [Character.AI](character_ai.md) is the largest library.
* If you want utility (research, code, drafts), [Claude](claude.md) and [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) outperform Replika by a wide margin - this isn't productivity software.

## FAQ

### Is Replika free?

Yes for basic chat. Pro is $19.99/mo or $69.99/year and unlocks voice calls, relationship modes (friend, mentor, sibling, partner), and advanced personality customisation. The free tier is enough to evaluate.

### Replika vs Character.AI - which one?

Different shapes. Replika is one persistent Rep that learns you over time. [Character.AI](character_ai.md) is thousands of community-made characters you talk to in shorter sessions. Pick Replika for relationship continuity, Character.AI for variety.

### Is Replika appropriate for everyone?

Hedge honestly: no. The product has been the subject of academic research on parasocial AI relationships, and content policy has shifted publicly multiple times. Approach with self-awareness about what AI companionship is and isn't.

### How good is the model behind Replika?

Weaker than frontier chat models like Claude and ChatGPT. Replika optimises for memory persistence and persona consistency, not raw reasoning. For productivity tasks, switch to a frontier model.
