# Sider: All-in-one AI sidebar across browsers and OSes

Sider is the multi-model AI sidebar that competes with [Monica](monica.md) for the "one extension, many models" niche, and the kitchen-sink alternative to direct frontier chats like [Claude](claude.md) or [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md). Sider is an all in one AI sidebar that I think of as Monica's louder sibling: same idea (one extension, many models), with a richer feature set and more aggressive product surface. It supports document chat, image generation, an AI writer, and a handful of agents, all behind a single subscription. For users who want every AI feature in one place rather than a stack of focused tools, it earns its keep.

## What it actually is

A browser extension and desktop app that bundles GPT, Claude, Gemini, and image generation into one assistant. Owned by a small Hong Kong based team. Chrome, Edge, and Firefox extensions plus macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android apps. Free tier with a daily prompt allowance; paid plans unlock higher limits and additional features.

## Setup

1. Install from [sider.ai](https://sider.ai) (extension or desktop app).
2. Sign in with Google, Microsoft, or email.
3. Pin to your browser toolbar; the sidebar opens with a hotkey or by clicking the icon.
4. (Optional) Connect Google Drive or Notion to chat with your documents.
5. (Optional) Subscribe to Pro for higher limits and access to the heavier models.

## How I use it day to day

* **PDF chat without uploading to a separate app.** Drag a PDF onto the sidebar; ask questions; close it when done. Lower friction than going to NotebookLM for a one off file.
* **Quick image generation.** Same sidebar; useful for thumbnails or social posts where I don't need Midjourney quality.
* **AI writer for short form.** Email replies, LinkedIn posts, that kind of thing. The output is fine; the value is being one click away while I'm already on the page.

I don't use Sider's agent features much; I'd rather drive an actual agent (Claude, Operator) for anything that matters.

## Gotchas

* Aggressive marketing in the UI. Lots of "upgrade now" prompts even on the paid tier.
* Quality of bundled features varies by model; the cheap defaults are worse than direct access to GPT or Claude.
* Like all multi model wrappers, you pay Sider's markup. Fine for casual users; expensive if you're using it many hours a day.
* Privacy: data flows through Sider's servers. Read the policy if any of your inputs are sensitive.

## Alternatives

* If you want the same "one sidebar, many models" idea with a quieter UI, [Monica](monica.md) is the closest alternative.
* If you want one strong assistant and no kitchen sink, [Claude](claude.md) or [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) directly is cleaner.
* If you want an AI-first browser instead of an extension, [Comet](comet.md), [Dia](dia.md), or [Sigma AI Browser](sigma_browser.md) are the options.
* If you want a privacy-first, built-in assistant, [Brave Leo](brave_search.md) ships inside the browser.

## FAQ

### Is Sider free?

Yes - the free tier covers a daily prompt allowance across the bundled models. Paid tiers (up to Sider Plus) lift limits and unlock heavier models. Heavy daily users tend to outgrow the free tier within a week.

### Sider vs Monica - which is better?

They overlap heavily. Both bundle GPT, Claude, Gemini, and image generation in a sidebar. Sider has a richer feature surface and louder UI; [Monica](monica.md) is calmer. Pick by which interface you find less noisy after a day of use.

### Does Sider replace ChatGPT Plus?

For casual use, maybe. For heavy use, no - the bundled defaults are weaker than direct access to GPT or Claude, and you pay Sider's markup on top. If you live in one model, subscribe to that model directly.

### Is Sider safe with sensitive data?

Data flows through Sider's servers for processing. The team is small and Hong Kong based. For regulated content or sensitive internal docs, read the privacy policy first or use a tool with stricter data guarantees.
