# Fyxer

Fyxer is the AI executive assistant for inbox triage. Where Superhuman teaches you keyboard speed and Shortwave gives you AI tools, Fyxer is more delegated — it categorises every email automatically, drafts responses in your voice, and surfaces only the messages that actually need your attention. The pitch is "treat your inbox like you have a chief of staff."

## What it actually is

A SaaS that connects to Gmail / Outlook and adds:
* **Auto categorisation** — every email tagged into folders (To respond, Awaiting reply, FYI, Newsletter, etc.).
* **Auto drafted responses** — Fyxer drafts a reply in your voice for emails needing one. You edit; click send.
* **Meeting notes integration** — captures meeting outcomes and follow ups in email.
* **Inbox dashboard** — daily morning summary of what matters.

## Setup

1. Go to [fyxer.com](https://www.fyxer.com), sign up.
2. Connect Gmail or Outlook (read + write access).
3. Pricing: Personal $30/mo, Pro $50/mo, Teams $100/seat/mo.
4. Onboarding takes ~10 minutes — Fyxer asks about your role, common topics, voice preferences.
5. The first day, watch Fyxer's categorisation and override misclassifications. Within a week, it converges on your patterns.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've tested Fyxer for a few weeks; effective for high inbox volume use cases, less so for low volume.
* **Triage at glance.** Fyxer's morning summary tells me what needs my response, what's awaiting reply, what can wait. Less time scrolling through 200 unread.
* **Drafted replies in voice.** Most routine emails get a Fyxer draft I edit lightly. Saves real minutes per email.
* **Newsletter handling.** Fyxer auto archives newsletters after summarising; I read the summary, only open the original if interested.
* **For high volume execs.** The product is shaped for hundreds of emails a day; below 50/day, the value is harder to feel.

## Gotchas

* Pricing is steep ($30/mo+). The argument is "saves you 5+ hours/week"; verify on your own usage.
* Fyxer learns from your past emails for voice matching. Privacy consideration: read the data policy.
* Drafted replies are starting points; never send blind. Fyxer can produce confidently wrong responses on nuanced topics.
* For collaborators on the same email account (shared inbox), Fyxer's per user voice training assumes individual accounts.
* For lower volume users, Superhuman's keyboard speed or Shortwave's free tier may be enough.
