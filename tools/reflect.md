# Reflect: networked notes with AI as your editor

Reflect sits in the notes and second-brain category alongside [Mem](mem.md), [Notion AI](notion_ai.md), and [Obsidian](obsidian.md). Reflect is the notes app with the most opinionated take on AI integration. Where Mem auto organises and Notion AI lives in your workspace, Reflect treats AI as your editor - a panel that knows about your notes and helps you think through them. Networked notes (bidirectional links); end to end encryption; daily notes as the default. The aesthetic is monastic; the workflow is for thinkers who write to think.

## What it actually is

A notes app available on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android, and web. Built around bidirectional links, daily notes, and AI augmentation. AI features include GPT‑4 backed conversations with your notes, AI suggested links, and AI assisted writing.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [reflect.app](https://reflect.app).
2. Pricing: $14/mo (annual) or $19/mo (monthly).
3. Install desktop and mobile apps.
4. Daily notes are the default landing page; create regular notes by typing `[[link name]]`.
5. AI panel: Cmd+J (or click the AI icon).

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I default to Obsidian for personal notes; Reflect is the alternative I'd recommend for non technical users who want bidirectional links without setup.
* **Daily note as the entry point.** Each day starts blank; I type fragments; bidirectional links emerge as I reference past notes.
* **AI chat with your notes.** "What did I think about X last quarter?" Reflect retrieves relevant notes; the AI summarises.
* **AI suggested links.** While writing, Reflect suggests existing notes the current text relates to. Builds the network without manual effort.
* **End to end encryption.** Notes are encrypted on the device; Reflect can't read them. The AI features run on encrypted data via clever architecture.
* **Calendar integration.** Pulls calendar events into daily notes for quick context.

## Gotchas

* No free tier. Subscription only.
* Smaller plugin / extension ecosystem than Obsidian.
* The AI works on your notes but the encryption + AI combination is novel; bugs occasionally. Read the docs on what "AI sees" exactly.
* For team or shared notes: Reflect is single user first. Notion / Coda fit team work better.
* Mobile experience is good but the canonical UX is desktop.

## Alternatives

* If you want OSS local-first networked notes with a huge plugin ecosystem, [Obsidian](obsidian.md) plus Smart Connections is the path.
* If you want self-organising notes with persistent memory, [Mem](mem.md) is the closer peer.
* If you want shared workspace and team notes with AI, [Notion AI](notion_ai.md) is the right shape.
* If you want object-based notes with AI baked in, [Capacities](capacities.md) is the alternative.

## FAQ

### Is Reflect free?

No - subscription only. $14/mo on annual or $19/mo on monthly. The pricing reflects the AI features and end-to-end encryption being included; expect to evaluate via the trial.

### Reflect vs Obsidian - which one?

Different audiences. [Obsidian](obsidian.md) is OSS, free, local-first, and has a massive plugin ecosystem - but you assemble your own setup. Reflect is opinionated, polished, and AI-native out of the box, at the cost of $168/year and a smaller ecosystem. Pick by whether you want to tinker.

### Does Reflect have end-to-end encryption?

Yes - notes are encrypted on the device. The AI features run on encrypted data via a clever architecture, but read the docs on what "AI sees" exactly before betting on it for sensitive notes. The combination is novel and bugs occasionally show up.

### Can multiple people share a Reflect workspace?

Not really. Reflect is single-user first; for team or shared notes, [Notion AI](notion_ai.md) or Coda fit better. Reflect treats notes as personal thinking surface.
