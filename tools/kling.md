# Kling: AI video with the best $/clip and longest durations

Kling is in the AI video generation category alongside [Veo](veo.md) and [Runway](runway.md), and it's the value pick - the lowest pricing and the longest per-clip durations of the major models. Kling is the AI video tool to use when you'd rather generate ten variations than one perfect clip. The pricing is the lowest of the major models, the durations are the longest (up to two minutes per clip), and the quality on V3 is genuinely competitive with Runway and Veo on motion physics. For creators iterating heavily, it's the obvious value pick.

## What it actually is

Kuaishou's text and image to video model. Available globally through [klingai.com](https://www.klingai.com). Generates clips up to 120 seconds long at 1080p, with V3 (April 2026) the current top tier. Strong at human motion (lipsync, body movement) and at long duration coherence.

## Setup

1. Go to [klingai.com](https://www.klingai.com), sign up with email or Google.
2. Free tier offers 166 daily credits (~3 short videos/day).
3. Pricing: Standard $6.99/mo (660 credits/mo), Premium $25.99/mo (3000 credits), Master $69.99/mo (9000 credits + early access).
4. Click Image to Video or Text to Video, fill the prompt, set duration (5s, 10s, up to 120s on Master), generate.

## How I use it day to day

* **Image to video at scale.** I generate 5 to 10 motion variations from the same still and pick the best. Cheap iteration is the point.
* **Long form takes.** For a single ~30 second establishing shot, Kling reaches there with one generation; Runway and Veo need stitching.
* **Lipsync.** Kling's character lipsync (separate feature) is among the best, useful for AI presenter videos.
* **Motion Brush** for region specific motion control. Comparable to Runway's, lower quality but free.
* **Comparing on benchmarks.** I include Kling in any image to video A/B because the price floor makes it easy.

## Gotchas

* English prompts work; the model is trained on Chinese language data so style references with Chinese cultural context land harder than Western equivalents.
* Quality varies more than Veo. Some generations are Runway tier; others are noticeably weaker. Generate variations.
* The web app's UI is functional but less polished than competitors. The translations are sometimes awkward.
* Credits expire; the monthly quota doesn't roll over.
* Kling is owned by Kuaishou (a Chinese company). Some enterprises have data residency concerns; check if relevant.

## Alternatives

* If you want the strongest all-rounder with native audio and prompt fidelity, [Veo](veo.md) is the default.
* If you want pro creative controls (motion brush, character refs, color), [Runway](runway.md) is the deeper toolset.
* If you want named cinematic camera moves, [Higgsfield](higgsfield.md) is more directorial.
* If you want a generous free tier for image-to-video, [Hailuo](hailuo.md) is the competitive freemium pick.

## FAQ

### Is Kling free?

Yes - the free tier offers 166 daily credits, enough for ~3 short videos/day. Paid: Standard $6.99/mo, Premium $25.99/mo, Master $69.99/mo. Master unlocks 120-second clips and early access.

### Kling vs Veo - which is better?

Different jobs. [Veo](veo.md) wins on overall quality, prompt fidelity, and native audio. Kling wins on price, clip length (up to 120s in one shot), and lipsync. For iteration-heavy work, Kling. For one polished shot, Veo.

### How long can a Kling video be?

Up to 120 seconds in a single generation on the Master tier. That's the longest of the major models in 2026 - everyone else (Veo, Runway, Pika) tops out shorter and needs stitching.

### Is Kling safe for enterprise data?

Kling is owned by Kuaishou, a Chinese company. Data residency may matter to your compliance team - check before uploading sensitive footage. If it's a hard no, [Veo](veo.md) (Google) or [Runway](runway.md) (US) are the alternatives.

### Does Kling do image-to-video?

Yes - and it's where Kling is most cost-effective. Generate 5-10 motion variations from the same still, pick the best. The cheap iteration is the actual value.
