# Talkie

Talkie is the mobile first AI roleplay app from Subllime / Minimax aligned developers, popular in the long tail of teen and young adult AI roleplay alongside Character.AI. It's character driven, aggressively visual (every companion has anime style art and short animated greetings), and built mobile first in a way that distinguishes it from web rooted competitors.

## What it actually is

A mobile AI roleplay and companion app. iOS and Android. Users create or pick from a marketplace of characters (mostly user generated), chat with them, and accumulate "soul" relationships. Heavy emphasis on visuals and on a card collection meta game. Free with optional in app purchases.

## Setup

1. Download from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Sign up with email, Google, or Apple.
3. Browse the character marketplace; pick a few that interest you.
4. Start chatting. Greeting messages are short animations.
5. (Optional) Create your own character: image, personality, backstory.
6. (Optional) Buy in app currency for premium features.

## How I use it day to day

I don't, day to day. The product's user base skews younger than mine, and the use case is recreational roleplay rather than utility. What I observe about why it works:

* **Mobile native UX.** Smoother on phones than Character.AI or Replika.
* **Visuals as the hook.** Animated greetings, character art, "soul" collection. Different design language from the more conversational competitors.
* **Marketplace momentum.** Lots of user generated characters, network effects similar to Character.AI's.

If you're evaluating companion apps for adults or for utility, this isn't the first one to try.

## Gotchas

* Content moderation has been controversial; the policies have shifted over time and remain a topic of public discussion. Verify the current state if it matters to you.
* In app purchases can stack up. Free tier is real but bounded.
* Privacy footprint is non trivial; chats may be visible to moderators or used for model improvement depending on settings.
* Roleplay quality is mid; the model isn't frontier grade, and that shows in long sessions.

## Pointers

* iOS and Android stores: search "Talkie AI."
