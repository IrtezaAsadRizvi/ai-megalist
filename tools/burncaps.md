# Burncaps: word-by-word captions and clips from a talking video, inside the browser

Burncaps sits beside [Submagic](submagic.md) and [Captions](captions.md) in video repurposing. It writes captions for a talking video and burns them in, or finds the best clips in a long recording and cuts each one to a vertical clip. The video itself never leaves the browser.

## What it actually is

A web app at [burncaps.app](https://burncaps.app/). Drop an MP4 or MOV: up to ten minutes for captions on the whole video, up to three hours for clips. Speech is heard on the device by Whisper or, for twelve Indian languages, IndicConformer, or by its speech service, which receives the sound only (phones use it by default; on a computer it is a choice). The words open as a script under the video: correct any word, then export an MP4 with captions burned in, or SRT, VTT, ASS or text files. Clips: about one for every two minutes of talk, up to 30, ranked by an AI model that reads the transcript text (never the video), or by a simpler rule on the device if you keep everything there; each is cut to 9:16 on the speaker, with captions. Captions in the language spoken or translated into any of 59 languages, 60 caption styles.

## Pricing

Free: 2 videos every 30 days, each with captions and up to 3 clips, no watermark, no sign-up. Then a 7-day Captions + Clips trial, no card. Captions + Clips is $29 a month for unlimited clips and captions; Captions is $9.98 a month or $49 once, for captioned videos only.

## Gotchas

Export is fastest in Chrome or Edge on a computer; Firefox may save a WebM file instead of an MP4, and many phones export too, more slowly. Long recordings are slower on slow machines because the work is done on yours.

## Alternatives

[Submagic](submagic.md) for B-roll and effects, [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md) and [Klap](klap.md) for other clip makers, [Descript](descript.md) for full editing.
