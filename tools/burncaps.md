# Burncaps: word-by-word captions burned into a talking video, inside the browser

Burncaps sits beside [Submagic](submagic.md) and [Captions](captions.md) in video repurposing. It writes captions for a talking video and burns them in, or cuts the best 30 or 60 seconds into a vertical clip. The video itself never leaves the browser.

## What it actually is

A web app at [burncaps.app](https://burncaps.app/). Drop an MP4 or MOV of up to ten minutes. Speech is heard by Whisper or, for twelve Indian languages, IndicConformer on the device, or by an optional speech service that receives the sound only. The words open as a script under the video: correct any word, then export an MP4 with captions burned in, or SRT, VTT, ASS or text files. 37 languages for translation, 20 caption styles.

## Pricing

Free to use: two videos every 30 days with no mark, or $49 once for unlimited videos. Studio is $29 a month for teams.

## Gotchas

Burning captions into the video needs Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc on a computer; Safari and Firefox can still make subtitle files. Long recordings are slower on slow machines because the work is done on yours.

## Alternatives

[Submagic](submagic.md) for B-roll and effects, [Opus Clip](opus_clip.md) to cut a long recording into many clips, [Descript](descript.md) for full editing.
