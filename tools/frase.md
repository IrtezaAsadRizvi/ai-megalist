# Frase

Frase is the SEO brief generator I'd hand to a freelance writer who needs to be productive on day one. Its output isn't the article; it's the brief: SERP analysis, suggested outline, related questions, key terms. That's exactly the artifact a writer needs to start, and Frase produces better briefs than most humans I've seen briefed by.

## What it actually is

An SEO content brief and writing platform. Given a keyword, Frase pulls top ranking pages, builds a research summary, generates an outline with header suggestions, and hands you a brief plus a content editor. There's also a generative AI module for drafting, but the brief workflow is the core. Web app with Google Docs export. Subscription pricing.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [frase.io](https://www.frase.io). Trial available.
2. Create a new document; enter a target keyword.
3. Frase analyses the top 20 SERPs and produces: a topic overview, an outline builder with competitor headings, a research panel of related questions and statistics, and a content editor.
4. (Optional) Use Frase AI to generate sections directly inside the editor.
5. (Optional) Export to Google Docs once the draft is ready for handoff.

## How I use it day to day

The brief is the deliverable, in my view, so:

* **Build the brief, hand to the writer.** Even if I never use Frase AI to generate prose, the brief itself is a great handoff artifact.
* **Use the related questions panel.** Frase surfaces "People Also Ask" style questions that often catch angles I'd miss writing cold.
* **Outline before drafting.** I copy Frase's suggested headers into a doc, edit them, and write from there.

For my own work I still write in plain text. For team content production, Frase is the kind of tool that makes a small team feel like a slightly larger one.

## Gotchas

* The AI generated content is fine, not great. Use the brief workflow; outsource the drafting to a frontier chat assistant or a human.
* SERP analysis only sees what's currently ranking. If a topic is changing fast, the brief lags.
* Pricing tiers gate certain features (especially Frase AI usage). Read the matrix; the cheapest tier may be sufficient for brief generation alone.
* Frase has been acquired and rebranded over the years; UI quirks remain.
