# DisconfirmAI: investment research and reading with counter-evidence

DisconfirmAI is an investment research and reading tool. It sits near [Perplexity](perplexity.md) and [ChatGPT Deep Research](chatgpt.md) in that it reads a lot of sources for you, but it is built for one job: checking a stock or theme idea against the evidence that could prove it wrong before you commit to it.

## What it actually is

A web app at [disconfirmai.com/en](https://disconfirmai.com/en) (Chinese version at [disconfirmai.com/zh](https://disconfirmai.com/zh)). It has five parts:

* **Reading.** A daily reading list drawn from public sources such as filings, company releases and news.
* **Research.** Digs into earnings reports and company data, and puts the evidence against an idea next to the case for it.
* **Radar.** You set conditions and it scans for changes that match them.
* **Investment framework.** Write down your own rules and check new ideas against them.
* **AI 100 and industry maps.** A ranked list of AI companies and maps of how industries fit together.

## Setup

1. Go to [disconfirmai.com/en](https://disconfirmai.com/en) and sign up.
2. Pricing: freemium. The free tier is enough to try Reading and Research.
3. Start with a company or idea you already hold and look at the counter-evidence section first.

## Gotchas

* It is a research aid. It does not give buy or sell calls.
* Coverage is strongest for listed companies with public filings.
* Built by a solo developer, so the product changes often.

## Alternatives

* For general cited web research, [Perplexity](perplexity.md) is broader.
* For working through your own documents, [NotebookLM](notebooklm.md) is the better fit.
