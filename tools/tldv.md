# tl;dv: meeting recorder with free unlimited transcription

tl;dv sits in the meeting notes category alongside [Fathom](fathom.md), [Fireflies](fireflies.md), and [Otter](otter.md), pitched as the bot-in-call recorder with a generous free tier and CRM sync for sales teams. tl;dv is the meeting recording / transcription / AI summary tool that started as a free Chrome extension and grew into a full meeting intelligence platform. The pitch: free unlimited recordings, AI summaries, CRM sync, and a search across all your meetings - Fathom adjacent in features, with its own personality.

## What it actually is

A meeting note taker (bot in call) that joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Free unlimited recording and transcription; paid tiers add AI summaries, CRM integration, and team features. Distinguishing features: timestamped highlights you can clip and share; speaker by speaker analytics; a custom AI assistant scoped to your meeting library.

## Setup

1. Go to [tldv.io](https://tldv.io), sign up with Google or Microsoft.
2. Free tier: unlimited recording + transcription. Many features beyond.
3. Pricing: Pro $29/seat/mo (AI summaries, CRM, custom templates), Business $98/seat/mo (more analytics, multi user).
4. Connect calendar; tl;dv offers to auto join meetings (toggleable).
5. After each meeting, the recording, transcript, summary, and highlights are in your dashboard.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I default to Granola for personal meeting notes; tl;dv is the alternative I'd point sales / customer success teams at.
* **Free unlimited recording** for individuals. Genuinely free; the team and CRM features are paid.
* **Highlight clips.** Mark a moment during the call (one keyboard shortcut); tl;dv saves a sharable clip with that timestamp. Useful for "send me where the customer mentioned competitor X."
* **Custom templates.** Configure summary structure per meeting type (sales call, 1:1, design review). Output reflects the template.
* **AI search across meetings.** "What did Sara say about pricing in March?" Searches across all your meeting transcripts.
* **CRM sync.** Pro tier pushes summaries and action items to Salesforce / HubSpot / Pipedrive automatically.

## Gotchas

* Bot in call ("tl;dv Notetaker") is visible; some attendees object.
* Free tier is generous but tier locked features (CRM, custom templates, AI search) are the differentiators for teams.
* Privacy: recordings live on tl;dv's cloud. Read residency docs for regulated industries.
* Quality of summaries depends on meeting structure; sprawling discussions produce less useful summaries.

## FAQ

### Is tl;dv free?

Yes for unlimited recording and transcription. Pro ($29/seat/mo) unlocks AI summaries, custom templates, and CRM sync; Business ($98/seat/mo) adds analytics and multi-user features.

### tl;dv vs Fathom - which is better?

Both have generous free tiers. [Fathom](fathom.md) leans simpler and consumer-friendly; tl;dv leans into sales workflows with CRM sync, custom templates, and cross-meeting AI search. Pick by which features you actually use.

### Does tl;dv work with Zoom and Google Meet?

Yes - it's a bot-in-call notetaker that joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. The bot is visible to attendees, which some object to; if that's a constraint, [Granola](granola.md) is bot-free.

### Can tl;dv push to Salesforce or HubSpot?

Yes, on Pro and above. Summaries and action items push to Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive automatically. Free tier doesn't include CRM sync.
