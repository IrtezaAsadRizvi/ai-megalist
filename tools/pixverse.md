# Pixverse: stylised and anime AI video

Pixverse is the stylised pick in the video generation category, where [Veo](veo.md), [Runway](runway.md), and [Kling](kling.md) chase photoreal. Pixverse is the AI video tool with the strongest output for stylised and anime motion. Where Veo / Runway aim at photoreal cinematic, Pixverse leans toward animation, anime, and stylised aesthetic. For creators making content where the look is "drawn moving" rather than "filmed," Pixverse hits a niche others don't.

## What it actually is

A web app at [pixverse.ai](https://pixverse.ai) with a text and image to video model focused on stylised aesthetics. The current version is V4 (April 2026); generates clips up to 8 seconds at 1080p, with strong support for anime, illustration, and game cinematic styles.

## Setup

1. Go to [pixverse.ai](https://pixverse.ai), sign up.
2. Free tier: 60 credits on signup; daily refresh of small credit allotment.
3. Pricing: Standard $14.90/mo (1200 credits), Premium $44.90/mo (4000), Ultimate $94.90/mo (10000).
4. Click Create. Pick text to video or image to video.
5. Type prompt; pick a style preset (Anime, Realistic, Cinematic, etc.); generate.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've tested Pixverse for evaluations; not a daily tool.
* **Anime motion.** The output captures anime aesthetic better than Veo / Runway in my testing. For creators making anime style shorts, Pixverse is the sharper tool.
* **Illustrated stills to motion.** Drop a Midjourney illustration; prompt motion. The result respects the source style well.
* **Game cinematic style.** Pre rendered "trailer for an indie game" looks; Pixverse hits this aesthetic on the first try more often than competitors.
* **Effects driven shots.** Magic, energy, particles - the stylised models handle these directly.
* **As one of the models in an A/B comparison** when the project's style isn't photoreal.

## Gotchas

* Photoreal quality is below Veo / Runway / Kling. Don't pick Pixverse for cinematic live action shots.
* Audio isn't natively generated. Add separately.
* Quality varies more than the "big three" video models. Generate variations.
* Some style presets are stronger than others; the anime presets are the most polished.
* Free tier is small but useful for evaluation; serious work needs paid.

## Alternatives

* If you want photoreal cinematic with native audio, [Veo](veo.md) is the all-rounder.
* If you need pro creative controls, [Runway](runway.md) is the right shape.
* If you need long durations at the best price, [Kling](kling.md) is the value pick.
* If you want playful named effects rather than anime, [Pika](pika.md) is the lighter alternative.

## FAQ

### Is Pixverse free?

Yes, with 60 credits on signup and a small daily refresh. Standard is $14.90/mo (1200 credits) and Premium $44.90/mo. Standard is the floor for serious anime / illustration work.

### Pixverse vs Veo - which is better?

Different aesthetics. [Veo](veo.md) wins on photoreal cinematic with native audio. Pixverse wins on anime, illustration, and game-cinematic styles. Pick by the look of the project.

### Does Pixverse generate anime well?

Yes - the anime presets are the most polished output and beat Veo / Runway in my testing for stylised motion. For "drawn moving" rather than "filmed," it's the sharper tool.

### Can Pixverse generate audio?

No - audio isn't natively generated. Add separately, either in a real editor or via [ElevenLabs](elevenlabs.md) for music and SFX.
