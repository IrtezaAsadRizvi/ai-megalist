# Read AI: cross-surface meeting and message summaries

Read AI sits in the meeting-notes category alongside [Granola](granola.md), [Otter](otter.md), and [Fathom](fathom.md), but stretches into email and chat too. Read AI is the meeting + email + messaging summary tool that tries to be your single AI assistant across every conversational surface. Meeting transcripts and summaries (like Otter); email summaries (like Shortwave); messaging app summaries (Slack, Teams chat). The pitch is that all your work conversations end up in one searchable AI memory.

## What it actually is

A platform with multiple integrations:
* **Meeting Copilot**: joins Zoom / Meet / Teams; transcribes + summarises.
* **Email Copilot**: Chrome extension for Gmail; summarises long threads.
* **Messaging Copilot**: Slack / Teams integration; summarises channels and DMs.
* **Search**: across all your captured conversations, ask questions in chat.

Free tier covers basics; paid tiers add CRM sync, longer retention, more integrations.

## Setup

1. Go to [read.ai](https://www.read.ai), sign up with Google or Microsoft.
2. Free tier: 5 meetings/mo + email summaries.
3. Pricing: Pro $19.75/mo, Enterprise + AI tiers higher.
4. Connect calendar; Read offers to auto join meetings.
5. Install Chrome extension for Gmail summaries.
6. Connect Slack / Teams for chat summaries (admin level setup).

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I tested Read AI for a few weeks; not my current default. The breadth is the pitch; for any single surface, more focused tools win.
* **Meeting summaries** comparable to Otter / Fathom. Quality is decent.
* **Email digest.** Daily summary of what came in, organised by topic. Useful when away from inbox for a few days.
* **Slack channel digests.** Catch up on a high traffic channel without scrolling. Summaries are reasonable for most channels.
* **Cross surface search.** "What did we say about pricing across all meetings, emails, and Slack?" The unified index is the unique value when it works.
* **Sentiment + speaker analytics** in meetings. Tracks talk time and emotional tone; useful for self review.

## Gotchas

* The breadth is the strength and the cost. A multi surface product tends to be weaker on each surface than focused alternatives.
* Privacy: Read sees all your meetings, emails, and chats. Significant trust ask. Read the data policy.
* CRM features are tier locked.
* Summaries on long sprawling threads / meetings are vague. Quality scales with structure.
* Cross surface insights are still rough; the "single memory" dream isn't fully realised.

## Alternatives

* If you only need meeting notes and want a bot-free flow, [Granola](granola.md) is the lighter pick.
* If you want free unlimited bot-based recording, [Fathom](fathom.md) is the cheaper path.
* If you want CRM-heavy meeting intelligence, [Fireflies](fireflies.md) is more sales-tuned.
* If you only want better email triage, [Superhuman](superhuman.md) or [Shortwave](shortwave.md) are stronger on that surface.

## FAQ

### Is Read AI free?

Yes, with 5 meetings/month plus email summaries. Pro is $19.75/mo and unlocks more meetings, longer retention, and cross-surface integrations. Enterprise tiers add admin and CRM features.

### Read AI vs Granola - which is better for meetings?

Different stances. Read AI joins as a bot and gives speaker analytics, sentiment, and CRM sync. [Granola](granola.md) stays out of the meeting and polishes your rough notes after. For meetings alone, focused tools win; Read AI's case is breadth across surfaces.

### Does Read AI summarise email and Slack?

Yes - that's the differentiator. Chrome extension for Gmail summaries, plus Slack and Teams integrations for channel digests. The cross-surface search ("what did we say about pricing across all meetings, emails, and Slack") is the unique value when it works.

### What's the privacy tradeoff?

Significant. Read AI sees your meetings, emails, and chats. Read the data policy carefully before standardising for regulated work or sensitive accounts.
