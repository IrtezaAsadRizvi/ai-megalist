# Higgsfield

Higgsfield is the AI video tool with cinematic camera presets baked in. Where most video models accept "make it cinematic" as a vague prompt, Higgsfield offers explicit moves — Bullet Time, Robo Arm, Crane Down, Whip Pan — with named, well executed implementations. For creators who think in shots, the directorial vocabulary is the differentiator.

## What it actually is

A web app at [higgsfield.ai](https://higgsfield.ai). Generates short video clips from text or image prompts, with the headline being a library of named camera moves. Underneath, multiple models (proprietary + partnered); the value is the curated, named, directional control.

## Setup

1. Go to [higgsfield.ai](https://higgsfield.ai), sign up.
2. Free tier: limited monthly credits.
3. Pricing: Basic $9/mo, Pro $29/mo, Ultimate $79/mo (more credits, advanced features).
4. Click Create. Pick a camera move (Bullet Time, Crane Up, etc.) or "Free" for no preset.
5. Type a prompt or upload an image; generate. A few seconds to ~60 seconds depending on length and model.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've tested Higgsfield's camera presets; not in production.
* **Bullet Time** for dramatic moments. Higgsfield's bullet time is the cleanest implementation outside of Hollywood VFX.
* **Crane shots.** Smooth vertical motion that feels professionally rigged. Hard to get right with prompt only on Veo / Runway.
* **Whip pan transitions** between scenes. Useful for short form video edits where you want stylised cuts.
* **As a complement to Veo / Runway.** When the project needs a specific camera move, Higgsfield does it more reliably; for general scenes, the bigger models are more flexible.
* **For social media B roll.** Stylised, attention grabbing motion; built for the algorithm.

## Gotchas

* The named presets are the value; off the rails prompts produce less impressive output.
* Quality varies by camera move. The popular ones (Bullet Time, Crane) are dialed in; some niche ones are weaker.
* Pricing per credit; complex shots cost more. Watch usage.
* For long takes or coherent narrative video across many shots, Higgsfield isn't the answer. It's a tool for individual cinematic moments.
* No native audio. Add SFX separately.
