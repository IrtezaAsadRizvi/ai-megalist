# Pika

Pika is the AI video tool with the playful streak. Pikaffects (drag a still in, click "explode it" or "melt it" or "inflate it"), Pikaframes (interpolate between two stills with a generated middle), Pikadditions (add an object to existing footage). The serious models (Veo, Runway) chase fidelity; Pika chases delight, and as a result the output is often the most shareable on social.

## What it actually is

A web based AI video platform from Pika Labs. Generates clips up to 10 seconds long, with the distinguishing features being the named effects rather than pure prompt to video quality. The current model is Pika 2.2 (April 2026); image to video, text to video, lipsync, and the family of "Pika" branded effects.

## Setup

1. Go to [pika.art](https://pika.art), sign up.
2. Free tier: 80 credits.
3. Pricing: Standard $10/mo (700 credits), Pro $35/mo (2300), Fancy $95/mo (6000 + faster generation, no watermark).
4. Click Create. Choose model, upload image (optional), type prompt or pick an effect.
5. Generate. ~30 to 90 seconds per clip.

## How I use it day to day

* **Pikaffects for social.** "Make this product explode." "Squish this." "Melt this." The output is novelty with high engagement potential.
* **Image to video.** Upload a still, prompt for motion. Comparable to Runway / Kling at slightly lower quality but cheaper.
* **Pikaframes** for transitions. Two images; Pika generates the in between motion. Useful for stylized transitions in edits.
* **Lipsync** for character driven shots. Decent though not best in class.
* **Quick experimentation.** Pika is the cheapest of the major video tools, which makes "throw a dozen ideas at it and see what sticks" affordable.

## Gotchas

* Quality is below Veo / Runway / Kling on naturalistic shots. Pika shines on stylised and effect driven work.
* The named effects can feel gimmicky after a few uses. Resist the urge to put Pikaffects in everything.
* Long form coherence (sequences that look connected) is harder; Pika is a single clip tool, not a story tool.
* Free tier is enough to evaluate; serious work needs Pro at minimum.
* Audio isn't generated natively. Add sound design separately.
