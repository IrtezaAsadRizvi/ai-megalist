# Kissable: AI companion with photo/video generation and community scenarios

Kissable is an AI companion app in the same category as [Replika](replika.md), [Nomi](nomi.md), and [Character.AI](character_ai.md), with photo/video generation and a community scenario catalog as the differentiators. Where most companions offer text and maybe voice, Kissable adds visual media generation and a creator ecosystem that gives it a different texture than the pure-chat alternatives.

## What it actually is

A consumer companion app (iOS, web) for creating an AI companion with persistent memory, emotional voice calls, and media generation. The core loop is conversation, but the companion can generate "together photos" (scenes with you and the AI), short video clips, and participate in community-created scenarios with branching storylines, NPCs, and lorebooks.

## Setup

1. Go to [kissable.app](https://kissable.app) or download from the [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759188783).
2. Sign up. 7-day free trial, then $19.99/mo for unlimited messages, voice, photo/video generation.
3. Customize your companion: appearance, personality, backstory.
4. Start chatting. Memory persists across sessions and grows with every conversation.
5. Explore the scenario catalog or create your own with the scenario creator tools.

## How it works day to day

* **Persistent memory is the foundation.** Like Nomi, the companion remembers across sessions. Mention something Tuesday, it comes back Thursday unprompted.
* **Photo generation is the unique feature.** Ask for a "together photo" and the AI generates an image of a scene with your companion. Not a gimmick - it shifts the emotional register of the interaction.
* **Video generation exists.** Short clips. Quality varies but the fact that it exists at all in a companion app is unusual.
* **Scenario catalog is the second differentiator.** Community-created scenarios with lorebooks, NPCs, branching paths. Think of it as a roleplay marketplace built into the companion.
* **Voice calls with emotional expressiveness.** Not just TTS - the voice adapts tone and emotion to context.

## Gotchas

* Paid-only after the trial. $19.99/mo with no free tier (aside from the 7-day trial). Pricier than some alternatives.
* iOS and web only - no Android app currently.
* Newer product than Replika or Character.AI - smaller community, less track record.
* Content policies exist but are more permissive than Character.AI.
* Same caveats as all companion apps apply: habit-forming by design, emotional attachment is real.

## Alternatives

* If you want memory and group chats without the media generation, [Nomi](nomi.md) is the closest comparison.
* If you want the largest character library and a free tier, [Character.AI](character_ai.md) has the volume.
* If you want the original gamified companion with wellness framing, [Replika](replika.md) has the longest track record.
* If you want lifelike personalities with social features, [Kindroid](kindroid.md) leans into that.

## FAQ

### Is Kissable free?

7-day free trial, then $19.99/mo. No permanent free tier. The trial gives full access to evaluate.

### Kissable vs Replika - which one?

Kissable if you want media generation (photos, videos) and scenario roleplay with community content. Replika if you want a more established product with gamified relationship progression. Different bets - Kissable leans into creative/visual; Replika leans into emotional support and wellness.

### Kissable vs Character.AI?

Character.AI has thousands of community characters and a free tier. Kissable has deeper single-companion relationships with memory, media generation, and scenario tools. Character.AI is breadth; Kissable is depth with one companion.

### Is the memory real?

Yes - persistent across sessions. Details from earlier conversations surface naturally in later ones. Similar to Nomi's approach.
