# Copy.ai

Copy.ai started in the same wave as Jasper but pivoted in a different direction: instead of leaning into brand voice and content marketing, they bet on "GTM AI" workflows for sales and revenue teams. The product today is more automation platform than copy generator, even though the name still says copy. For sales ops people who want AI in their lead enrichment and outbound flows, it's a meaningful contender.

## What it actually is

A go to market workflow platform for sales and marketing teams. Includes long form content generation, but the bigger surface is workflow automations: lead enrichment, account research, outbound personalization, content repurposing. SaaS web app with API and Zapier / Slack integrations. Subscription pricing.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai). Free starter tier; paid tiers for team use.
2. Pick a use case: content generation, GTM workflow, or custom.
3. For workflows: connect data sources (CRM, lead lists, web data) and pick a template like "research this account and draft an outbound email."
4. (Optional) Bring in your team; configure role based access.
5. (Optional) Connect to Slack or your CRM for triggered runs.

## How I use it day to day

I've used Copy.ai mostly for the GTM workflow side. For sales adjacent tasks (account research, draft outbound, summarize a long lead profile) the templated workflows are faster than building the same thing in Claude with a custom prompt every time.

* **Account research.** Drop in a company URL, get a structured brief with news mentions, tech stack guesses, and likely buyer titles.
* **Outbound personalization.** Take a lead list, get personalized opener variants per lead.
* **Content repurposing.** A blog post becomes ten LinkedIn posts. Quality is fine; I'd still edit before publishing.

For pure short form copy I don't reach for Copy.ai over a frontier chat assistant. The workflows are the differentiator.

## Gotchas

* The product has shifted GTM heavy in the last two years. If you're a content marketer, [jasper.md](jasper.md) or [writer.md](writer.md) may fit better.
* Workflow quality depends on data quality. Bad lead lists produce bad emails, regardless of the model.
* Output requires human review. Sales prospects can spot a wholly automated email instantly; the value is hours saved on first drafts, not zero touch automation.
* Pricing has moved upmarket; the entry tier is less generous than it used to be.

## Pointers
