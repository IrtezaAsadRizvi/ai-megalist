# Copy.ai: GTM workflow platform for sales and marketing

Copy.ai is a marketing / GTM-automation tool, sitting alongside [Jasper](jasper.md) and [Writer](writer.md) in the marketing AI category - but bet on workflow automation rather than brand-voice copy. It started in the same wave as Jasper but pivoted in a different direction: instead of leaning into brand voice and content marketing, they bet on "GTM AI" workflows for sales and revenue teams. The product today is more automation platform than copy generator, even though the name still says copy. For sales ops people who want AI in their lead enrichment and outbound flows, it's a meaningful contender.

## What it actually is

A go to market workflow platform for sales and marketing teams. Includes long form content generation, but the bigger surface is workflow automations: lead enrichment, account research, outbound personalization, content repurposing. SaaS web app with API and Zapier / Slack integrations. Subscription pricing.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai). Free starter tier; paid tiers for team use.
2. Pick a use case: content generation, GTM workflow, or custom.
3. For workflows: connect data sources (CRM, lead lists, web data) and pick a template like "research this account and draft an outbound email."
4. (Optional) Bring in your team; configure role based access.
5. (Optional) Connect to Slack or your CRM for triggered runs.

## How I use it day to day

I've used Copy.ai mostly for the GTM workflow side. For sales adjacent tasks (account research, draft outbound, summarize a long lead profile) the templated workflows are faster than building the same thing in Claude with a custom prompt every time.

* **Account research.** Drop in a company URL, get a structured brief with news mentions, tech stack guesses, and likely buyer titles.
* **Outbound personalization.** Take a lead list, get personalized opener variants per lead.
* **Content repurposing.** A blog post becomes ten LinkedIn posts. Quality is fine; I'd still edit before publishing.

For pure short form copy I don't reach for Copy.ai over a frontier chat assistant. The workflows are the differentiator.

## Gotchas

* The product has shifted GTM heavy in the last two years. If you're a content marketer, [jasper.md](jasper.md) or [writer.md](writer.md) may fit better.
* Workflow quality depends on data quality. Bad lead lists produce bad emails, regardless of the model.
* Output requires human review. Sales prospects can spot a wholly automated email instantly; the value is hours saved on first drafts, not zero touch automation.
* Pricing has moved upmarket; the entry tier is less generous than it used to be.

## Alternatives

* If you want brand voice and templated marketing copy, [Jasper](jasper.md) is the dedicated tool that Copy.ai stepped away from.
* If you want enterprise content governance and brand consistency at scale, [Writer](writer.md) is the right shape.
* If you want SEO-first content with SERP analysis, [Surfer SEO](surfer_seo.md) or Frase fits better.
* For lighter outbound personalisation without a workflow platform, a [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) or [Claude](claude.md) project with your team's playbook usually matches the output.

## FAQ

### Is Copy.ai free?

There's a free starter tier. Paid plans target teams; pricing has moved upmarket from the early days, and the free tier is less generous than it used to be. Free is enough to evaluate templates; serious GTM use needs paid.

### Copy.ai vs Jasper - which should I use?

Different bets now. [Jasper](jasper.md) doubled down on brand voice and marketing copy. Copy.ai pivoted to GTM workflow automation (lead enrichment, outbound). For content marketing, Jasper. For sales ops, Copy.ai.

### Can Copy.ai replace a frontier chat assistant for content?

For pure short-form copy, no - a [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) or [Claude](claude.md) project with your style guide usually matches the output. The differentiator now is the workflow side: pre-built templates for lead research, outbound personalisation, and content repurposing pipelines.

### Does Copy.ai integrate with my CRM?

Yes - Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack are the headline integrations, with Zapier coverage for the long tail. The workflow value depends on these being wired in; standalone use loses most of the point.
