# Jamie: bot-free meeting notes for EU teams

Jamie is in the meeting notes category alongside [Granola](granola.md) and [Fathom](fathom.md), and it's the GDPR-friendly bot-free option I'd point European teams at. Jamie is the bot free meeting assistant for European data conscious teams. Where Granola popularised the "no bot in call" model, Jamie has been doing it longer with a deeper focus on EU privacy norms - GDPR compliant, data hosted in the EU, no recordings stored after transcription unless you choose to. For companies in regulated European markets, Jamie is the safer choice.

## What it actually is

A desktop app for macOS and Windows. Captures audio locally during meetings (via system audio + microphone, no bot joining the call); transcribes; produces structured notes. Integrations include CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive), Slack, Teams, Notion. Pricing in EUR.

## Setup

1. Download from [meetjamie.ai](https://www.meetjamie.ai).
2. Install. Grant audio permissions (system audio + mic).
3. Sign up. Free tier: 10 meetings.
4. Pricing: Standard €24/mo (40 meetings), Pro €37/mo (unlimited), Business €47/mo (custom prompts, team features).
5. Hit "Start meeting" before a call. Jamie captures locally; cloud transcription is optional.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've tested Jamie alongside Granola; for non EU users, my preference goes to Granola for UX. For EU specific needs, Jamie is the choice.
* **Bot free capture.** No "Jamie Notetaker" joining the call; nothing for attendees to object to.
* **GDPR compliant by design.** Data stays in the EU; recordings are optional; deletion is straightforward.
* **Meeting notes.** Comparable quality to Granola / Otter post processing summary.
* **Custom prompts.** Tell Jamie what kind of summary to produce ("focus on decisions and action items" or "include questions raised"). Per meeting type.
* **CRM sync** for sales workflows.

## Gotchas

* Lower brand recognition outside Europe; not a barrier, just less community content.
* Free tier (10 meetings) is enough to evaluate; daily use needs paid.
* Local audio capture means anything not picked up by your mic / system (separate device, in person speaker) is missed.
* The summary quality depends on meeting structure. Long sprawling discussions produce vaguer summaries.
* For team scale collaboration features, Pro and Business tiers add what you'd need.

## Alternatives

## FAQ

### Is Jamie free?

Yes for evaluation - the free tier covers 10 meetings. Standard at €24/mo (40 meetings) and Pro at €37/mo (unlimited) are the realistic floors for daily use; Business at €47/mo adds custom prompts and team features.

### Jamie vs Granola - which is better?

Different defaults. [Granola](granola.md) is the cleaner UX overall in my testing; Jamie is the safer pick if your data residency rules require EU hosting and GDPR-by-design. Both are bot-free.

### Is Jamie GDPR compliant?

Yes - data is hosted in the EU, recordings are optional and deletable, and capture happens locally on your machine without a bot joining the call. That's the core sales pitch.

### Does Jamie work without a bot in the call?

Yes - Jamie captures system audio plus your microphone locally. No "Jamie Notetaker" joins as a participant, which is the point for privacy-conscious meetings.

### What does Jamie miss?

Anything your machine can't hear: a separate device on the call, an in-person speaker not picked up by your mic, audio routed through hardware Jamie doesn't see. Bot-based tools catch some of this; bot-free doesn't.
