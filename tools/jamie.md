# Jamie

Jamie is the bot free meeting assistant for European data conscious teams. Where Granola popularised the "no bot in call" model, Jamie has been doing it longer with a deeper focus on EU privacy norms — GDPR compliant, data hosted in the EU, no recordings stored after transcription unless you choose to. For companies in regulated European markets, Jamie is the safer choice.

## What it actually is

A desktop app for macOS and Windows. Captures audio locally during meetings (via system audio + microphone, no bot joining the call); transcribes; produces structured notes. Integrations include CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive), Slack, Teams, Notion. Pricing in EUR.

## Setup

1. Download from [meetjamie.ai](https://www.meetjamie.ai).
2. Install. Grant audio permissions (system audio + mic).
3. Sign up. Free tier: 10 meetings.
4. Pricing: Standard €24/mo (40 meetings), Pro €37/mo (unlimited), Business €47/mo (custom prompts, team features).
5. Hit "Start meeting" before a call. Jamie captures locally; cloud transcription is optional.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've tested Jamie alongside Granola; for non EU users, my preference goes to Granola for UX. For EU specific needs, Jamie is the choice.
* **Bot free capture.** No "Jamie Notetaker" joining the call; nothing for attendees to object to.
* **GDPR compliant by design.** Data stays in the EU; recordings are optional; deletion is straightforward.
* **Meeting notes.** Comparable quality to Granola / Otter post processing summary.
* **Custom prompts.** Tell Jamie what kind of summary to produce ("focus on decisions and action items" or "include questions raised"). Per meeting type.
* **CRM sync** for sales workflows.

## Gotchas

* Lower brand recognition outside Europe; not a barrier, just less community content.
* Free tier (10 meetings) is enough to evaluate; daily use needs paid.
* Local audio capture means anything not picked up by your mic / system (separate device, in person speaker) is missed.
* The summary quality depends on meeting structure. Long sprawling discussions produce vaguer summaries.
* For team scale collaboration features, Pro and Business tiers add what you'd need.
