# Todoist

Todoist is the task manager that's been around long enough to be both classic and modern. The 2024–25 AI features (AI Assistant, smart task suggestions, natural language task creation) layered on top of an already excellent productivity app made it more capable without disrupting what worked. For people who organise their lives in a task list, Todoist + AI is one of the smaller upgrades to a tool you already know.

## What it actually is

A task management app for web, desktop (macOS, Windows, Linux), iOS, and Android. Features include projects, sections, labels, filters, recurring tasks, natural language input, comments, sub tasks, calendar sync, and now AI Assistant. The free tier is generous; Pro adds integrations and AI features.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [todoist.com](https://todoist.com).
2. Free tier: 5 active projects, basic features.
3. Pricing: Pro $5/mo (1 user, 300 projects, AI Assistant), Business $8/seat/mo.
4. Install desktop / mobile apps.
5. Add tasks via the quick add: type "Pay rent every 1st of month #Finance @phone" — Todoist parses date, project, label.
6. (AI) Hover over a task → AI Assistant for suggestions.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Todoist for years pre AI; the AI features are useful, not essential.
* **Natural language task entry.** Quick add bar parses dates, recurrence, projects, labels. Faster than dropdown menus.
* **Filters as queries.** "Today & p1" shows urgent today; "@waiting & 7 days" shows blocked items unchecked for a week. Powerful.
* **AI Assistant.** Click on a task; the AI suggests how to break it into sub tasks, what could go wrong, what context might be missing. Light touch; useful for project tasks.
* **Smart suggestions** for parsing email or text into tasks. Forward an email; Todoist proposes a task with the right details.
* **Karma + streaks** as habit reinforcement. Gamified; opt out if you find that annoying.

## Gotchas

* Pro's AI features are limited compared to dedicated AI tools (ChatGPT for breakdown). The integration is the value.
* For complex project management (gantt charts, dependencies, team workflows), Todoist isn't the answer. Asana, ClickUp, Linear fit better.
* Free tier is real but the project cap (5 active) hits hobbyists fast.
* Some integrations (Calendar two way sync, Outlook) are tier locked.
