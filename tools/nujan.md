# Nujan: dual-pane scientific paper reader with Socratic coaching and multi-paper synthesis

Nujan sits in the academic PDF reading and literature synthesis space alongside [Explainpaper](explainpaper.md), [SciSpace](scispace.md), and [NotebookLM](notebooklm.md). It pairs a full-scroll native PDF reader (with a Zotero-style dark mode that inverts page glare while keeping figures and plots in original color) next to a side-by-side AI chat, Socratic Coach, and Sparring Partner.

## What it actually is

A web workspace at [nujan.app](https://nujan.app) built for graduate students and researchers reading dense technical papers.

* **Split-screen PDF reader.** Upload a local PDF (up to 100 MB) or import directly via an arXiv ID or DOI link. Highlight any equation, algorithm block, or methodology paragraph on the PDF to ask questions in the side panel or branch into a 2D spatial knowledge graph.
* **Zotero-style PDF dark mode.** Darkens the white PDF page sheet (`#171717`) while rotating figure and diagram hues back to true color so late-night reading does not cause eye strain.
* **Socratic Coach & Sparring Partner.** Instead of just summarizing, Coach mode walks through mathematical derivations and prerequisite concepts step-by-step with checkpoints, while Sparring Partner mode stress-tests hypotheses, experimental designs, and missing baselines like a critical peer reviewer.
* **Self-organizing library & slide decks.** Automatically renames uploaded PDFs to canonical publication titles, extracts concept tags, synthesizes multi-paper RAG comparisons, and exports Reveal.js, Marp, or LaTeX Beamer slide decks for journal clubs.

## Setup

1. Open [nujan.app](https://nujan.app). Ten pre-loaded landmark papers across subfields (Transformers, DDPMs, AlphaFold, Fourier Neural Operators, MACE force fields, LFADS neural dynamics, Double/Debiased ML, Diffusion Policy, Generative Agents, and Quantum error correction) run interactively in the browser without creating an account.
2. Sign in with Google to save papers to your personal library (starts with complimentary welcome credits, no card required).
3. Drag and drop a PDF or paste an arXiv URL/ID into the library bar.
4. Open the paper in the Split Reader, highlight any passage or equation, and choose `Ask in Chat`, `Branch`, or `Note`.

## Pricing

* Free tier: includes starter credits on sign-up (no card), single PDF uploads, auto-renaming, auto-tagging, TL;DR extraction, and the in-app dark-mode PDF reader (up to 50 stored papers).
* Pro ($3.99/month): 1,500 monthly credits, single-paper and library-wide RAG chat, batch uploads, citation exports, and paper-to-slide deck generation (up to 1,000 stored papers).
* Pro+ ($7.99/month): 4,000 monthly credits, Socratic AI Coach, AI Sparring Partner, multi-paper slide decks, and conference intelligence (up to 5,000 stored papers).

## Where it fits day to day

* Unpacking dense mathematical derivations, proofs, or methodology sections right next to the original two-column PDF without copy-pasting broken LaTeX into a separate chatbot tab.
* Reading papers at night with true-color figures in PDF dark mode.
* Entering an unfamiliar subfield using the Socratic Coach to build a prerequisite roadmap before reading a target paper.
* Preparing a 10-minute weekly lab meeting or journal club presentation directly from a paper PDF.

## Gotchas

* **Web-first workflow.** There is no native iOS/Android app or offline desktop client yet; it runs in the browser.
* **Credit-metered deep reasoning.** While PDF reading, dark mode, and metadata organization are free, multi-turn Socratic Coaching, Sparring, and RAG chat consume credits.
* **Zotero two-way sync is not live yet.** You can export citations (BibTeX, APA, MLA, Chicago) and import PDFs/arXiv links, but automatic two-way Zotero collection syncing is still on the roadmap.

## Alternatives

* If you just want quick highlight-to-explain on a single passage without library organization or Socratic coaching, [Explainpaper](explainpaper.md) is simpler.
* If you want a broad paper discovery database coupled with PDF chat and literature tables, [SciSpace](scispace.md) and [Elicit](elicit.md) have larger built-in search indices.
* If you want to drop 10 PDFs into a notebook and generate an audio podcast overview for your commute, [NotebookLM](notebooklm.md) is the best fit.
