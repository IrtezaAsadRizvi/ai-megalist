# Photoroom

Photoroom is the AI image editor for product photography and commerce. The mobile and web apps remove backgrounds, generate scenes for products, batch process catalogs, and fit into the e commerce workflow without making you learn Photoshop. For sellers (Etsy, eBay, Shopify), the time savings on listing photos are immediate.

## What it actually is

A mobile app (iOS, Android) plus web app. Specialised in:
* **Background removal** — among the highest accuracy in the field.
* **AI Backgrounds** — generate scene backgrounds matching your product (e.g. "marble countertop" for a kitchen tool).
* **AI Shadows** — realistic drop shadows for products.
* **Batch processing** — remove backgrounds, resize, recolor across hundreds of photos.
* **Templates** — pre built layouts for marketplaces (Amazon, Shopify, Etsy).

## Setup

1. Download from App Store / Google Play, or use [photoroom.com](https://www.photoroom.com).
2. Free tier: removed backgrounds with watermark on certain features.
3. Pricing: Pro $9.99/mo, Business $19.99/mo (batch + API), Enterprise.
4. Snap or upload a product photo; tap remove background; choose a backdrop or AI generate one.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Photoroom for personal listings (kid's outgrown toys, Marketplace sells); not a daily tool.
* **Mobile workflow.** Phone photos of a product → background removed → AI generated lifestyle scene → ready to post. Two minutes start to finish.
* **AI Backgrounds.** Type a description; Photoroom generates a coherent scene with realistic perspective behind your product. Particularly strong on products with clear silhouettes.
* **Batch processing.** Hundreds of product photos with the same treatment. Pro tier handles batches; saves hours.
* **Templates** for marketplaces. Auto resize, auto crop, brand consistent overlays. Cuts the per listing prep time.
* **Shadows that look right.** Generated drop shadows that match the lighting of the AI background. Subtle, important.

## Gotchas

* AI backgrounds work best on clearly outlined products; transparent / wispy objects (hair, glass) can have weird edges.
* Pro tier is the floor for serious use. Free tier is for evaluation.
* Output resolution caps differ by tier; for print quality, check before committing.
* For service or non product photography, Photoroom's commerce focus is overkill.
* The API (Business tier) is solid for embedding into your own listing tools.
