# Bricks

Bricks is a spreadsheet built around AI prompts as the primary interaction. Where Rows added AI to a spreadsheet, Bricks designed the spreadsheet around AI from day one: every cell can be the result of a prompt, every chart can be generated from a description, every workflow can be automated by a request. It's a smaller product than the Excel and Sheets giants, with a more distinctive bet.

## What it actually is

An AI native spreadsheet web app. Cells, charts, and workflows can all be generated from natural language prompts. Includes integrations with common business APIs and document import. Subscription pricing.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [thebricks.com](https://www.thebricks.com). Trial available.
2. Start a new sheet; either type cell formulas as usual, or describe what you want and let the AI generate.
3. Generate charts and visualisations from a description.
4. (Optional) Import data from CSV, Excel, or Sheets.
5. Share with a team or publish.

## How I use it day to day

I haven't shipped serious work with Bricks; the demos are compelling and my casual experiments have been promising:

* **Quick analysis from a CSV.** Upload, ask "show me top customers by revenue, split by region." The chart appears. Faster than building it manually for the simple cases.
* **Prompt to formula.** When I'd otherwise google "how do I do an INDEX MATCH for X," asking Bricks in plain English is faster.
* **Document to spreadsheet.** Pasting a PDF table or a meeting transcript and asking for structured output works well in casual use.

For real modeling I still go to Excel. For team collaboration, Sheets. Bricks is the bet on a different shape of spreadsheet, and it's interesting to watch.

## Gotchas

* AI generated formulas are confidently wrong sometimes. Always verify before relying on a number.
* The product is smaller than Excel or Sheets; expect feature gaps.
* Pricing model has shifted as the product matures; check current tiers.
* As an AI native product, your data flows through their inference path. Read the privacy policy if anything is sensitive.
