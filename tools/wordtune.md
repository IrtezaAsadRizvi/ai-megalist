# Wordtune

Wordtune is the AI rewriting tool that respects your voice. Unlike "rewrite this" prompts in ChatGPT (which often produce generic sanitised prose), Wordtune offers targeted rewrites — shorten, expand, formal, casual, "make this sound smarter" — and shows you a few options to pick from. For non native English writers, copywriters, and anyone who polishes their drafts, Wordtune is the lower friction option.

## What it actually is

A browser extension + web app + iOS / Android app from AI21 Labs. Originally a single feature ("rewrite this sentence"); now bundles Wordtune Rewrite, Wordtune Read (summarise long content), Wordtune Spices (specific mods like add fact, add example, counter argument), and Wordtune Editor (full document editor with AI everywhere).

## Setup

1. Install the Chrome / Edge / Safari extension from [wordtune.com](https://www.wordtune.com).
2. Sign up. Free tier: 10 rewrites/day, 3 reads/week.
3. Pricing: Plus $9.99/mo, Premium $14.99/mo, Team $14.99/seat/mo.
4. Highlight any text in any web app (Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Slack, etc.); the Wordtune button appears.
5. Click → choose Rewrite / Shorten / Expand / Casual / Formal → pick from 3 to 5 alternatives.

## How I use it day to day

* **Email rewrites.** "Make this less stiff" or "make this more direct." Wordtune offers a few takes; pick the best.
* **Tightening LinkedIn / Twitter posts** under the character limit. Shorten with multiple options is the workflow.
* **Translation polish.** Non native writers translate to English; Wordtune polishes phrasing without losing meaning.
* **Counterpoint.** Wordtune Spices has "add counterpoint" — useful when a draft feels one sided.
* **As a sanity check.** Wordtune's options show me what other phrasings exist; I sometimes keep my original after seeing alternatives.
* **Lighter touch than ChatGPT.** Wordtune doesn't take over my voice; it offers variants of what I wrote.

## Gotchas

* The free tier limit (10/day) is restrictive for daily use. Plus is the floor.
* Quality of rewrites varies by length. Short sentences: great. Long paragraphs: sometimes loses nuance.
* The browser extension doesn't work in every web app; some text fields it can't reach.
* For full document editing: Lex or Google Docs with Gemini are more polished editor experiences.
* For long form generation: Sudowrite / ChatGPT / Claude. Wordtune is the rewriter, not the writer.
