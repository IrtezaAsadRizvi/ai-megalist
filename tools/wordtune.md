# Wordtune: AI rewriter that preserves your voice

Wordtune sits in the writing category alongside [Grammarly](grammarly.md) and [Lex](lex.md), but specifically as the targeted rewriter (not the grammar checker or full editor). Wordtune is the AI rewriting tool that respects your voice. Unlike "rewrite this" prompts in ChatGPT (which often produce generic sanitised prose), Wordtune offers targeted rewrites - shorten, expand, formal, casual, "make this sound smarter" - and shows you a few options to pick from. For non native English writers, copywriters, and anyone who polishes their drafts, Wordtune is the lower friction option.

## What it actually is

A browser extension + web app + iOS / Android app from AI21 Labs. Originally a single feature ("rewrite this sentence"); now bundles Wordtune Rewrite, Wordtune Read (summarise long content), Wordtune Spices (specific mods like add fact, add example, counter argument), and Wordtune Editor (full document editor with AI everywhere).

## Setup

1. Install the Chrome / Edge / Safari extension from [wordtune.com](https://www.wordtune.com).
2. Sign up. Free tier: 10 rewrites/day, 3 reads/week.
3. Pricing: Plus $9.99/mo, Premium $14.99/mo, Team $14.99/seat/mo.
4. Highlight any text in any web app (Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, Slack, etc.); the Wordtune button appears.
5. Click → choose Rewrite / Shorten / Expand / Casual / Formal → pick from 3 to 5 alternatives.

## How I use it day to day

* **Email rewrites.** "Make this less stiff" or "make this more direct." Wordtune offers a few takes; pick the best.
* **Tightening LinkedIn / Twitter posts** under the character limit. Shorten with multiple options is the workflow.
* **Translation polish.** Non native writers translate to English; Wordtune polishes phrasing without losing meaning.
* **Counterpoint.** Wordtune Spices has "add counterpoint" - useful when a draft feels one sided.
* **As a sanity check.** Wordtune's options show me what other phrasings exist; I sometimes keep my original after seeing alternatives.
* **Lighter touch than ChatGPT.** Wordtune doesn't take over my voice; it offers variants of what I wrote.

## Gotchas

* The free tier limit (10/day) is restrictive for daily use. Plus is the floor.
* Quality of rewrites varies by length. Short sentences: great. Long paragraphs: sometimes loses nuance.
* The browser extension doesn't work in every web app; some text fields it can't reach.
* For full document editing: Lex or Google Docs with Gemini are more polished editor experiences.
* For long form generation: Sudowrite / ChatGPT / Claude. Wordtune is the rewriter, not the writer.

## Alternatives

* If you want grammar-and-spelling first with broad app coverage, [Grammarly](grammarly.md) is the established pick.
* If you want a clean AI-native long-form editor, [Lex](lex.md) is the editor experience to try.
* If you want fiction-specific rewriting (Story Engine, Canvas), [Sudowrite](sudowrite.md) is purpose-built.
* If you want general-purpose rewriting with full conversation context, [Claude](claude.md) or [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) preserve voice well on longer text.

## FAQ

### Is Wordtune free?

Yes - 10 rewrites/day and 3 reads/week. Plus is $9.99/mo, Premium $14.99/mo. The free tier limit hits fast for daily use; Plus is the realistic floor.

### Wordtune vs Grammarly - which should I use?

Different jobs. [Grammarly](grammarly.md) catches grammar and spelling errors; Wordtune offers stylistic rewrites (shorten, expand, formal, casual). Many writers run both side by side - they don't overlap much.

### Does Wordtune work in Gmail and Google Docs?

Yes - it's a browser extension that activates on selected text in most web apps (Gmail, Docs, LinkedIn, Slack). Some text fields it can't reach; the surface coverage is wide but not universal.

### Wordtune vs ChatGPT for rewriting?

Wordtune is lighter touch. [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) often takes over your voice when asked to rewrite; Wordtune offers 3-5 alternatives that stay close to your original phrasing. For polish, Wordtune. For substantive rewrites, ChatGPT or [Claude](claude.md).
