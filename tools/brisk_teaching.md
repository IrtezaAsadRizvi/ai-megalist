# Brisk Teaching

Brisk Teaching is the AI extension built specifically for K 12 teachers, sitting inside Google Docs, Slides, and Drive. It does what the general purpose AI tools do (summarize, generate, give feedback) but framed and scoped for classroom workflows: leveling reading passages, generating quizzes, giving rubric aligned feedback on student writing. It's a verticalized AI product, and the vertical fit is the value.

## What it actually is

A Chrome extension and web app for teachers, by Brisk Teaching Inc. Integrates with Google Workspace (Docs, Slides, Forms) and a growing list of LMS platforms. Free tier; paid tiers for power users and schools.

## Setup

1. Install the Brisk extension from the Chrome Web Store.
2. Sign in with a Google account (school or personal).
3. Open Google Docs, Slides, or Forms; the Brisk sidebar appears on demand.
4. Pick an action: change reading level, generate quiz, give feedback, write an IEP, etc.
5. (Optional) Subscribe for higher generation limits and additional features.

## How I use it day to day

I'm not a teacher; the educators I've talked to who use it report:

* **Reading level adjustment.** Take a passage; rewrite at a 6th grade level (or 3rd, or 9th). Useful for differentiated instruction without manual rewrites.
* **Quiz generation.** Source a Google Doc; produce a quiz aligned to it. Saves a lot of busywork.
* **Feedback on student writing.** Brisk gives rubric aligned comments; teachers review and tweak before sharing.
* **Lesson plan drafts.** Frames the prompt right for the K 12 audience; better than asking ChatGPT cold.

For teachers, the value is having a tool that already understands the workflow. Calling Claude directly is more flexible but requires building the prompt engineering yourself.

## Gotchas

* Output requires teacher review. AI generated quizzes can have wrong answers, generated feedback can miss context.
* Privacy and student data: read the policy carefully if you're using it on student work. School level deployment usually addresses this; individual teacher use less so.
* Free tier limits are generous but the more useful flows tend to be on paid plans.
* The product is K 12 oriented; higher ed tools (Cohort or Khanmigo for tutoring) fit different audiences.
