# NovelAI: fiction-tuned models with lorebook continuity

NovelAI is an AI fiction writing service in the same category as [Sudowrite](sudowrite.md) and [NovelCrafter](novelcrafter.md), distinguished by running its own custom-finetuned fiction models rather than wrapping frontier APIs. NovelAI is the original AI fiction writing service, born in the GPT 3 era and built around its own custom finetuned models. Where NovelCrafter and Sudowrite wrap frontier models, NovelAI runs models specifically tuned on fiction (Erato is the current flagship), with an editor designed for the rhythm of storytelling rather than the rhythm of marketing copy. It has a niche but devoted user base.

## What it actually is

An AI assisted creative writing service by Anlatan. Includes a custom finetuned LLM for fiction (Erato), a story editor with memory and lorebook features, an AI image generator (NovelAI Diffusion), and a text to speech voice option. Subscription pricing; no free tier beyond a brief trial.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [novelai.net](https://novelai.net). Pick a tier (Tablet, Scroll, Opus); Opus unlocks the full feature set.
2. Open the editor; create a new story.
3. Configure context: memory (a static prompt for tone and setting), authors note (a dynamic instruction the model sees nearby), and lorebook (entries triggered by keywords).
4. Start writing. Hit tab (or the equivalent) to generate the next chunk; accept, edit, or regenerate.
5. (Optional) Use NovelAI Diffusion for inline image generation tied to the story.

## How I use it day to day

I'm not a regular user, but the writers who use NovelAI describe a few advantages over the wrapper products:

* **Models tuned for fiction.** Erato has a different feel from frontier chat models; it's been trained on prose rather than instruction tuned for assistance. Some writers prefer this; others don't.
* **Privacy stance.** Anlatan publishes its data policy and is positioned as not training on user content. Writers working on sensitive or original IP often cite this as the reason they pay.
* **Lorebook continuity.** Triggered context entries handle long form continuity without needing the full codex apparatus.

## Gotchas

* Models are the differentiator and they lag the absolute frontier on raw quality. If you want the smartest possible writing partner, Claude or ChatGPT win.
* The UX is functional but quirky; older feeling than the newer entrants.
* Image generation is decent but specialized; for general image work, Midjourney or Flux are stronger.
* Community has a strong opinion about which settings work; expect to spend time tuning sliders.

## Alternatives

* If you want a more polished UX with frontier models doing the writing, [Sudowrite](sudowrite.md) is the category leader.
* If you want a structured codex / world-bible for novel-length continuity, [NovelCrafter](novelcrafter.md) is more codex-focused.
* If you want a generic frontier chat for shorter creative work, [Claude](claude.md) handles prose well.
* If image generation tied to story scenes is the draw, [Midjourney](midjourney.md) is stronger on imagery alone.

## FAQ

### Is NovelAI free?

No, there's a brief trial but no permanent free tier. Tablet, Scroll, and Opus tiers escalate context length and feature access; Opus is the practical default for serious users.

### NovelAI vs Sudowrite - which is better?

NovelAI when you want a fiction-tuned model and a privacy stance about not training on your text. [Sudowrite](sudowrite.md) when you want the most polished modern UX and frontier model quality. Different bets - NovelAI is custom; Sudowrite is wrapped.

### Does NovelAI train on my writing?

Anlatan publishes its data policy as not training on user content - that's part of the pitch for writers working on sensitive or original IP. Read the current policy directly; positions in this space shift.

### Is NovelAI good in 2026?

Erato (the current flagship model) is solid but lags frontier general-purpose models on raw quality. Devotees say the fiction tuning is worth the gap; if you want the smartest possible writing partner, [Claude](claude.md) or [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) win on capability.
