# NovelAI

NovelAI is the original AI fiction writing service, born in the GPT 3 era and built around its own custom finetuned models. Where NovelCrafter and Sudowrite wrap frontier models, NovelAI runs models specifically tuned on fiction (Erato is the current flagship), with an editor designed for the rhythm of storytelling rather than the rhythm of marketing copy. It has a niche but devoted user base.

## What it actually is

An AI assisted creative writing service by Anlatan. Includes a custom finetuned LLM for fiction (Erato), a story editor with memory and lorebook features, an AI image generator (NovelAI Diffusion), and a text to speech voice option. Subscription pricing; no free tier beyond a brief trial.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [novelai.net](https://novelai.net). Pick a tier (Tablet, Scroll, Opus); Opus unlocks the full feature set.
2. Open the editor; create a new story.
3. Configure context: memory (a static prompt for tone and setting), authors note (a dynamic instruction the model sees nearby), and lorebook (entries triggered by keywords).
4. Start writing. Hit tab (or the equivalent) to generate the next chunk; accept, edit, or regenerate.
5. (Optional) Use NovelAI Diffusion for inline image generation tied to the story.

## How I use it day to day

I'm not a regular user, but the writers who use NovelAI describe a few advantages over the wrapper products:

* **Models tuned for fiction.** Erato has a different feel from frontier chat models; it's been trained on prose rather than instruction tuned for assistance. Some writers prefer this; others don't.
* **Privacy stance.** Anlatan publishes its data policy and is positioned as not training on user content. Writers working on sensitive or original IP often cite this as the reason they pay.
* **Lorebook continuity.** Triggered context entries handle long form continuity without needing the full codex apparatus.

## Gotchas

* Models are the differentiator and they lag the absolute frontier on raw quality. If you want the smartest possible writing partner, Claude or ChatGPT win.
* The UX is functional but quirky; older feeling than the newer entrants.
* Image generation is decent but specialized; for general image work, Midjourney or Flux are stronger.
* Community has a strong opinion about which settings work; expect to spend time tuning sliders.
