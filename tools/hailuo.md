# Hailuo

Hailuo (also called MiniMax Hailuo) is the AI video model with the most generous free tier in the field. Free users get a meaningful number of generations per day, the quality on image to video is genuinely competitive, and the credits roll over reasonably. For creators evaluating AI video without committing to a paid tool, Hailuo is the path of least resistance.

## What it actually is

A text and image to video model from Chinese AI lab MiniMax. Available at [hailuoai.com](https://hailuoai.com) (or through partner platforms). Generates clips up to 6 seconds at 1080p (longer with stitching), with image to video being particularly strong. The current model lineup includes T2V‑01, I2V‑01, and a director controlled variant.

## Setup

1. Go to [hailuoai.com](https://hailuoai.com), sign up.
2. Free tier: ~100 credits/day.
3. Paid tiers: Standard $14.90/mo (1000 credits), Unlimited $94.90/mo. Annual discounts.
4. Click Create. Pick a model (T2V or I2V); upload image (if I2V); type prompt; generate.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've tested Hailuo for comparisons; it's a credible choice especially for image to video.
* **Image to video.** Drop a still, prompt motion. The model preserves source style well; output looks close to the input frame.
* **Free tier generations** for evaluation. More than any major competitor offers.
* **Director mode.** Specific camera moves and motion controls; the syntax is "[zoom in] subject smiles [pan right]" embedded in the prompt. Powerful when learned.
* **As a value option.** When I want lots of variations cheap, Hailuo's per video cost on the Standard tier is among the lowest.

## Gotchas

* Quality is strong on some prompts and noticeably weaker on others. Generate variations; pick the best.
* Audio isn't natively generated. Add separately.
* The web UI is functional but less polished than Veo / Runway. Translation in some menus is awkward.
* Free tier resets daily but doesn't accumulate; if you don't use today's credits, they're gone tomorrow.
* MiniMax is a Chinese company; data residency concerns apply for some enterprises.
