# Fireflies: meeting bot wired into the CRM

Fireflies is in the meeting-notes category alongside [Granola](granola.md), [Fathom](fathom.md), and [Otter](otter.md), positioned squarely at sales / revenue teams who want every call flowing into their CRM automatically. Where Otter focuses on transcription quality and Granola on the bot-free experience, Fireflies optimises for "what happens to the transcript afterward." Native CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot), playlist creation, conversation analytics, knowledge base - Fireflies is for organisations that want their meetings to flow into their systems automatically.

## What it actually is

A meeting bot + analytics platform. Joins Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, BlueJeans as a participant; transcribes; summarises; pushes data to your downstream tools. Features include speaker identification, sentiment analysis, action items, custom topic tracking, conversation intelligence dashboards, AI assistant ("AskFred").

## Setup

1. Go to [fireflies.ai](https://fireflies.ai), sign up with Google or Microsoft.
2. Free tier: 800 minutes of transcription/mo, basic features.
3. Pricing: Pro $18/seat/mo, Business $29/seat/mo (custom apps, sentiment, more storage), Enterprise.
4. Connect calendar; Fireflies offers to auto join meetings (configurable per meeting).
5. Connect integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Asana). Most are admin level setups.
6. Mobile app for in person meeting recording.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Fireflies in a sales adjacent role. For non sales contexts, simpler tools are sufficient.
* **CRM auto sync.** Sales call ends; transcript + summary + action items posted to the contact in HubSpot. Reps don't update CRMs reliably; Fireflies fixes that.
* **Topic tracking.** Configure topics (e.g. "competitor mentioned," "pricing concern"); dashboards show frequency across meetings. Useful for revenue teams.
* **AskFred (their AI assistant).** "Show me all calls this quarter where the customer mentioned [feature]." Searches across the meeting library.
* **Soundbites and Playlists.** Clip key moments; build playlists for sales coaching or onboarding.
* **Action item extraction.** Decent. The "decisions made" feature is the more useful one for non sales meetings.

## Gotchas

* Fireflies joins as a visible participant. Same etiquette concerns as other bot based tools.
* Sales focused features are tier locked. The free tier is fine for personal transcription; CRM integrations live at Business+.
* Quality of summaries varies with meeting structure. Sprawling discussions produce vague summaries.
* Privacy: recordings live on Fireflies' cloud. Read the data residency docs for regulated industries.
* Compete area is crowded - for non sales, [granola.md](granola.md), [fathom.md](fathom.md), [otter.md](otter.md) all overlap.

## Alternatives

* If you don't need CRM auto-sync and just want fast personal meeting notes, [Granola](granola.md) is bot-free and faster to set up.
* For unlimited free recording with simpler CRM hooks, [Fathom](fathom.md) is the better personal default.
* If transcription quality and OtterPilot integrations matter more than analytics, [Otter](otter.md) has been doing this longest.
* For deep sales coaching analytics on calls (deal risk, talk-ratio benchmarks), Gong and Chorus are the enterprise-priced upgrades.

## FAQ

### Is Fireflies free?

Yes - free tier covers 800 minutes of transcription/mo with basic features. Pro is $18/seat/mo, Business $29/seat/mo (CRM, custom apps, sentiment), Enterprise on top. The CRM integrations most people sign up for live at Business+.

### Fireflies vs Otter - which is better?

Different tilts. [Otter](otter.md) is sharper on transcription quality and live captions; Fireflies is sharper on what happens after the call (CRM sync, topic tracking, AskFred search). For sales teams, Fireflies. For pure note-taking, Otter is fine.

### Does Fireflies integrate with HubSpot and Salesforce?

Yes, both, plus Notion, Slack, Asana. CRM auto-sync is the headline Business-tier feature. Setup is admin-level (OAuth + field mapping); plan an hour for the first integration.

### What is AskFred?

Fireflies' built-in AI assistant that searches across your meeting library. Ask "show me all calls this quarter where the customer mentioned [feature]" and it returns the relevant clips. Quality is decent on well-tagged libraries; degrades on ambiguous queries.
