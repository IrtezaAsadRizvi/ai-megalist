# Gong: revenue intelligence built on sales conversations

Gong is what happens when you record every sales call your team makes, transcribe it, and run AI over the corpus to figure out which pitches actually convert. It's the de-facto pick for B2B revenue teams - not because the AI is the best in the world, but because the dataset (your own sales calls + everyone else's, aggregated) is uniquely Gong's. If you're in any role that touches a sales motion, you've probably been on a Gong-recorded call.

## What it actually is

A revenue intelligence platform from Gong.io. Records and transcribes sales calls, demos, and customer conversations across Zoom, Meet, Teams, and phone systems. Layers AI on top: deal warnings, coaching suggestions, automated CRM updates, forecast intelligence, and a chat surface ("Gong Engage" / "Gong AI") for asking questions across the call corpus. Enterprise-priced and sales-led; market leader in this space.

## Setup

1. This is a sales-led purchase - book a demo at gong.io. Expect a 5+ figure annual contract.
2. Once licensed, IT will wire up the meeting integrations (Zoom, Meet, Teams) and CRM connector (Salesforce, HubSpot).
3. Reps install the desktop / browser app; Gong joins or post-processes meetings.
4. Set up the workspace: deal stages, scorecards, coaching plans, AI alerts.
5. Use the Gong web app for daily ops; managers use Forecast and Deal Boards.

## How I use it day to day

(Use cases are typical of revenue ops teams; this isn't a tool for individual contributors outside sales.)

* **Deal review** with AI-summarized highlights from every call on an opportunity.
* **Coaching** - pull the calls where reps lost the deal at the pricing stage; review patterns.
* **Forecast roll-up** with AI-flagged risks (e.g., "champion hasn't replied in 14 days, deal at risk").
* **Cross-deal Q&A** - "summarize objections about feature X this quarter."

## Gotchas

* Recording requires consent. Compliance setup matters; check the local laws (US two-party-consent states, EU GDPR).
* The price tag is enterprise. SMBs reach for [Fathom](fathom.md), [Avoma](https://www.avoma.com), or [Otter](otter.md) instead.
* The "AI" layer has improved fast but isn't frontier - core value is the dataset and the workflow integration, not the model.
* Implementation takes weeks. Don't expect to flip a switch and see ROI day one.

## Alternatives

* **Avoma** - mid-market alternative; meeting assistant + deal coaching, lower price floor.
* **Chorus (ZoomInfo)** - the other revenue-intelligence heavyweight; bundled with ZoomInfo prospecting.
* [Fathom](fathom.md) - cheaper, simpler meeting recorder; less deal-intelligence.
* [Granola](granola.md) - founder/PM-focused note-taker; not sales-specific.
* [Otter](otter.md) - general-purpose meeting transcription.

## FAQ

### Is Gong free?

No - sales-led enterprise pricing. Expect $1,500+/user/year.

### Gong vs Chorus?

Chorus is owned by ZoomInfo and tightly bundled with their prospecting suite. Gong is independent and arguably has the better standalone product. Most large orgs evaluate both.

### Gong vs Avoma?

[Avoma](https://www.avoma.com) is the SMB / mid-market alternative - similar shape, lower floor, less mature for very large teams.

### Can I use Gong without a CRM?

You can, but you'll lose 80% of the value. The CRM integration is core.

### Does Gong have an AI assistant?

Yes - "Gong AI" / "Engage" lets reps and managers query across the call corpus in chat. Useful for the kind of cross-deal questions a single meeting transcript can't answer.
