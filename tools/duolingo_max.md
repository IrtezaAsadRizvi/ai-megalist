# Duolingo Max

Duolingo Max is the AI tier of Duolingo. The two distinguishing features — Roleplay (have a conversation in your target language with an AI partner) and Explain My Answer (the bird tells you *why* you got it wrong) — are the kind of thing every language learner has wanted forever and could never quite get from a static app.

## What it actually is

A subscription tier (~$30/mo, $13/mo annual) on top of regular Duolingo. Built on GPT‑4 and successors, with prompts tuned for language learning at the user's CEFR level. The same Duolingo app, with two AI features added.

## Setup

1. Open Duolingo (iOS, Android, web).
2. Settings → Subscription → upgrade to Max. Free trial available.
3. The two AI features appear in lessons:
   * **Explain My Answer:** after a wrong answer, tap the explanation icon for a personalised breakdown.
   * **Roleplay:** unlocks scenarios like "order at a French café" or "negotiate a hotel reservation in Spanish." Type or speak; the AI responds in role.
4. (Optional) Video Call — talk to Lily (AI tutor) by video for free conversation practice.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Duolingo (regular) for ~3 years and Max for ~6 months on Spanish.
* **Roleplay daily.** 5 minutes of conversational practice every morning. The AI doesn't judge me for grammatical mistakes the way a real tutor might, which lowers the barrier to actually speaking.
* **Explain My Answer for tricky grammar.** Subjunctive vs indicative — instead of just learning "you got it wrong," the explanation walks through why each form would or wouldn't work.
* **Video Call with Lily.** Ten minute open ended conversations on a topic of my choosing. Less polished than Roleplay but useful for unscripted practice.
* **Not a replacement for real practice.** Duolingo Max is good complementary input. For fluency you still need a human conversation partner; Max delays the "I need a real tutor" point but doesn't eliminate it.

## Gotchas

* Available languages for Max are fewer than regular Duolingo. Spanish, French, English (for non English speakers), German, Japanese, etc. — check the list.
* The AI features only appear inside the Duolingo course structure. You can't have an arbitrary Spanish conversation; it's tied to lesson content.
* Roleplay scenarios repeat after enough cycles. The path lengths exist but aren't infinite.
* Pricing is high vs alternatives. Speak ($14/mo) is conversation focused and may be a better value for pure speaking practice.
* Voice recognition is decent but not flawless. The AI sometimes misses you, which produces awkward exchanges.

## Pointers

* [duolingo.com](https://www.duolingo.com)
* For pure conversation practice with strong speech recognition: [Speak](https://www.speak.com).
* For listening comprehension at level: pair Duolingo with podcasts in your target language at slow speed.
* Real human conversation is still the gold standard. Italki + Duolingo Max is a strong combination.
