# Duolingo Max: AI tier for language practice

Duolingo Max is a language-learning tool in the education category, the AI tier sitting head-to-head with [Speak](speak.md) for conversational practice. The two distinguishing features - Roleplay (have a conversation in your target language with an AI partner) and Explain My Answer (the bird tells you *why* you got it wrong) - are the kind of thing every language learner has wanted forever and could never quite get from a static app.

## What it actually is

A subscription tier (~$30/mo, $13/mo annual) on top of regular Duolingo. Built on GPT‑4 and successors, with prompts tuned for language learning at the user's CEFR level. The same Duolingo app, with two AI features added.

## Setup

1. Open Duolingo (iOS, Android, web).
2. Settings → Subscription → upgrade to Max. Free trial available.
3. The two AI features appear in lessons:
   * **Explain My Answer:** after a wrong answer, tap the explanation icon for a personalised breakdown.
   * **Roleplay:** unlocks scenarios like "order at a French café" or "negotiate a hotel reservation in Spanish." Type or speak; the AI responds in role.
4. (Optional) Video Call - talk to Lily (AI tutor) by video for free conversation practice.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Duolingo (regular) for ~3 years and Max for ~6 months on Spanish.
* **Roleplay daily.** 5 minutes of conversational practice every morning. The AI doesn't judge me for grammatical mistakes the way a real tutor might, which lowers the barrier to actually speaking.
* **Explain My Answer for tricky grammar.** Subjunctive vs indicative - instead of just learning "you got it wrong," the explanation walks through why each form would or wouldn't work.
* **Video Call with Lily.** Ten minute open ended conversations on a topic of my choosing. Less polished than Roleplay but useful for unscripted practice.
* **Not a replacement for real practice.** Duolingo Max is good complementary input. For fluency you still need a human conversation partner; Max delays the "I need a real tutor" point but doesn't eliminate it.

## Gotchas

* Available languages for Max are fewer than regular Duolingo. Spanish, French, English (for non English speakers), German, Japanese, etc. - check the list.
* The AI features only appear inside the Duolingo course structure. You can't have an arbitrary Spanish conversation; it's tied to lesson content.
* Roleplay scenarios repeat after enough cycles. The path lengths exist but aren't infinite.
* Pricing is high vs alternatives. Speak ($14/mo) is conversation focused and may be a better value for pure speaking practice.
* Voice recognition is decent but not flawless. The AI sometimes misses you, which produces awkward exchanges.

## Alternatives

* If you mainly want speaking practice with strong speech recognition, [Speak](speak.md) is the dedicated conversation tool.
* If you want general AI tutoring beyond languages (with a calmer voice), [Khanmigo](khanmigo.md) is the broader K-12 tutor.
* If you want to chat freely in your target language without lesson scaffolding, [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) Voice mode or [Claude](claude.md) handles it well.
* For real human conversation practice, italki paired with Duolingo Max is the strong combination.

## FAQ

### Is Duolingo Max worth $30/mo?

Depends on how much you use it. If you do 5+ minutes of Roleplay daily, the value-per-day is reasonable. If you mainly do regular Duolingo lessons, Super tier (cheaper) covers most needs. Annual pricing ($13/mo) is the sane way to buy.

### Duolingo Max vs Speak - which is better?

Different shapes. [Speak](speak.md) is purpose-built for speaking practice with stronger speech recognition; lessons are conversation-first. Max bolts AI onto the existing Duolingo course structure - the gamification and habit-formation are the unique value. For pure speaking, Speak. For sustained habit, Duolingo.

### Can Duolingo Max replace a real tutor?

No. It's good complementary input - lowers the barrier to actually speaking, fills the daily-practice gap. For fluency you still need a human conversation partner who can correct you in real-time. Max delays the "I need a tutor" point but doesn't eliminate it.

### What languages does Max support?

Spanish, French, English (for non-English speakers), German, Japanese, and a growing list. Smaller than regular Duolingo's full catalog. Check the Duolingo site before paying - if your target language isn't on Max, you're back to the standard tier.
