# Beautiful.ai

Beautiful.ai is the deck tool with strong opinions about design. Where Gamma generates whole decks from a prompt and PowerPoint lets you do anything (including bad things), Beautiful.ai gives you smart templates that auto adjust as you add content. The slide knows what it's supposed to look like; you focus on the message. The AI features (DesignerBot for slide generation) are layered on top of that opinionated foundation.

## What it actually is

A presentation tool with smart slide templates that automatically rebalance as you add content, plus an AI generator (DesignerBot) for whole deck creation from a prompt. Web based. Subscription pricing for individual and team use.

## Setup

1. Sign up at [beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai). Free trial.
2. Start a new deck: blank, from a template, or via DesignerBot prompt.
3. Pick a theme; this controls fonts, colours, and spacing across the whole deck.
4. Add slides from the smart slide library (chart, timeline, image grid, etc.). Each one rebalances as you type.
5. (Optional) Use DesignerBot for first draft content; iterate from there.

## How I use it day to day

* **Internal decks.** Status updates, board presentations. Beautiful.ai's smart slides keep me from spending an hour on alignment.
* **Charts and data.** The chart slides update as I edit data; cleaner than Excel or Sheets exports.
* **Quick first drafts.** DesignerBot for a 10 slide outline; then I rewrite for voice.

For client facing pitch decks where every pixel matters, I still go to Figma or Keynote. Beautiful.ai is for the 80% of decks where template intelligence is more valuable than full control.

## Gotchas

* The opinionated layout sometimes fights you. If you have a slide that doesn't fit any of the smart templates, the workaround feels clunky.
* DesignerBot output is generic. Use it to skip the empty page; rewrite for substance.
* Export to PowerPoint loses some of the auto rebalancing magic. Plan to live in Beautiful.ai if you'll be editing.
* Pricing has crept up; confirm current tiers before subscribing.

## Pointers

* Web: [beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai)
* Pricing: monthly tiers; team plans for collaboration.
* Pairs and competes with [gamma.md](gamma.md) (more aggressive AI generation, less template focused) and [tome.md](tome.md). For visual storytelling beyond decks, [canva.md](canva.md) is broader.
