# AIVA: AI music generator for cinematic and orchestral scoring

AIVA sits in the music generation cluster next to [Suno](suno.md), [Udio](udio.md), and [Stable Audio](stable_audio.md), but aimed at instrumental scoring rather than pop songs. AIVA is the AI music tool for film, game, and trailer scoring. Where Suno generates pop songs and Mubert generates loops for backgrounds, AIVA generates orchestral / cinematic / classical pieces in the structure of actual music - verses, bridges, dynamics, motifs that develop. For composers wanting a sketching tool and content creators needing scored music, AIVA is the credible answer.

## What it actually is

A web app at [aiva.ai](https://www.aiva.ai). Generates instrumental music in 250+ styles (cinematic, electronic, jazz, ambient, fantasy, etc.). Outputs include MIDI, MP3, and stems; you can edit in AIVA's piano roll or export to Logic / Ableton / FL Studio. Recognised as the first AI to register copyrights on its compositions in some jurisdictions.

## Setup

1. Go to [aiva.ai](https://www.aiva.ai), sign up.
2. Free tier: 3 downloads/mo, MP3 only, attribution required.
3. Pricing: Standard €15/mo (15 downloads, no attribution), Pro €49/mo (300 downloads, full ownership of compositions).
4. Click Create Track. Pick a style, set length, key, time signature.
5. AIVA generates a track; iterate with regenerate, edit in piano roll, export.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used AIVA for one off scoring; not regularly.
* **Trailer / promo music.** Cinematic build with clear sections. AIVA's style range covers most "epic" needs.
* **Game music sketches.** Generate themes for areas / characters; bring into a real DAW for production polish.
* **Editing in piano roll.** AIVA's piano roll lets me tweak the AI generated MIDI. Closer to "co writing" than other AI music tools.
* **Stems for mixing.** Export individual instruments for control in Logic or Ableton.
* **Pro tier** for clear ownership rights when commercial use is intended.

## Gotchas

* No vocals. Instrumental only.
* Output is more "competent stock music" than "memorable theme." For something that lands, plan to edit substantially.
* MIDI export is real and editable; MP3 export is mastered but locked. Use MIDI for serious work.
* Pricing tier gates ownership rights. Read terms carefully if commercial release matters.
* For pop / songwriting with vocals: [suno.md](suno.md), [udio.md](udio.md). For atmospheric / SFX: [stable_audio.md](stable_audio.md).

## Alternatives

* If you want full songs with vocals and the most polished UX, [Suno](suno.md) is the category leader.
* If you want high control over song structure and don't mind ongoing legal uncertainty, [Udio](udio.md) is the comparator.
* If you want atmospheric pads and SFX rather than scored music, [Stable Audio](stable_audio.md) is closer.
* If you just want adaptive ambient soundscapes (focus, sleep), [Endel](endel.md) is built around that single use case.

## FAQ

### Is AIVA free?

The free tier covers 3 downloads/month with attribution required and MP3 only. Standard at €15/mo lifts you to 15 downloads with no attribution; Pro at €49/mo gives full ownership of compositions, which is what you need for commercial release.

### AIVA vs Suno - which is better?

Different jobs. [Suno](suno.md) makes pop songs with vocals; AIVA makes instrumental cinematic and orchestral pieces with editable MIDI. Pick by whether you want a song or a score.

### Can I use AIVA tracks commercially?

Tier-dependent. Pro (€49/mo) grants full ownership of compositions; lower tiers retain ownership constraints. Read the terms before commercial release.

### Does AIVA export MIDI?

Yes - MIDI plus stems plus mastered MP3. The MIDI export is the reason composers actually use it; you get to bring it into Logic or Ableton and rewrite the parts that don't land.
