# Character.AI: AI roleplay platform with the largest character library

Character.AI sits in the personal companions cluster alongside [Replika](replika.md), [Nomi](nomi.md), and [Pi](pi.md), differentiated by the size of its community-built character library and a generous free tier. Character.AI is the largest AI roleplay platform by a wide margin and the entry point most people have to "talk to an AI as a character." Tens of thousands of community made characters cover everything from historical figures to fictional ones to generic listeners. The free tier is genuinely usable, which is the rare combination that drives the user base.

## What it actually is

A web and mobile app for creating and chatting with AI characters. Each "character" is a system prompt + persona + (optional) voice + (optional) image. You can create characters yourself or chat with public ones. The default models have been the company's own; recent versions also support voice conversations and group chats with multiple characters.

## Setup

1. Go to [character.ai](https://character.ai) or download the iOS/Android app.
2. Free tier is the default. There's a c.ai+ paid tier ($9.99/mo) for faster responses and priority access.
3. Browse characters by category, or create your own:
   * Click Create → Character.
   * Fill in name, greeting, definition (system prompt), avatar, voice.
   * Save and chat.
4. Memory works per chat by default; some features (Pinned Memories) expand this.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I tested Character.AI for ~5 hours to write this; it's not a daily tool for me. The user base for it is enormous and I'm not the median user.
* **Roleplay scenarios.** Practice difficult conversations (negotiation, conflict, public speaking) with characters built for the situation.
* **Niche characters.** Want to "talk to" Marcus Aurelius about a question? Someone has built him. The community library is the surface.
* **Group chats** with multiple characters. Useful for narrative writing - let the characters interact with each other and with you.
* **Voice mode** for hands free conversation. Good driving / walking companion if the character is well chosen.

## Gotchas

* Content policies were tightened in 2024–25 in response to safety concerns; some users moved to alternatives.
* The default Character.AI models are noticeably weaker than frontier models (GPT, Claude). Don't expect Claude's reasoning.
* Memory across long roleplays drifts. Pinned Memories help but aren't bulletproof.
* The free tier has rate limits; popular characters can be slow at peak times.
* The platform is consumer first; for serious creative writing or professional applications, the limits show fast.

## Alternatives

* If you want stronger long-term memory and group chats with deeper personalisation, [Nomi](nomi.md) is the comparator.
* If you want the original gamified relationship-app framing, [Replika](replika.md) is the long-running alternative.
* If you want a calmer conversational tone without roleplay framing, [Pi](pi.md) is the closest non-character option.
* If you want the smartest model behind your character (frontier reasoning), build a Custom GPT in [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) or a Project in [Claude](claude.md) instead.

## FAQ

### Is Character.AI free?

Yes - the default tier is free and genuinely usable. c.ai+ at $9.99/mo gets you faster responses and priority access during peak times.

### Character.AI vs ChatGPT - what's the difference?

Different products. Character.AI's models are weaker than frontier models like [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) or [Claude](claude.md), and you can't expect their reasoning. Character.AI wins on character library and casual roleplay; ChatGPT wins on intelligence.

### Is Character.AI safe for kids?

Content policies were tightened in 2024-25 in response to safety concerns. Even after the changes, parental review is sensible - the platform is consumer-first and minor users have caused real incidents. Read the company's safety updates.

### How long is Character.AI's memory?

Per-chat memory by default. Pinned Memories expand this but aren't bulletproof - long roleplays drift over time. For deeper memory, [Nomi](nomi.md) is the alternative.

### Can I build my own characters?

Yes - Create -> Character. Fill in name, greeting, definition (system prompt), avatar, optionally a voice. Save and chat. The community library is built this way.
