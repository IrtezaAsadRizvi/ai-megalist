# Pi

Pi was Inflection AI's bet on a personal AI built around emotional intelligence and conversational warmth rather than utility. It launched in 2023, attracted serious users for its calm tone and active listening style, and changed shape after Inflection's leadership and core team moved to Microsoft in 2024. The product is still online and still distinctive; just operating in a smaller orbit than its early hype suggested.

## What it actually is

A personal AI assistant by Inflection AI. Available at [pi.ai](https://pi.ai), as iOS and Android apps, and via WhatsApp / Messenger / SMS in some regions. Tone first design: Pi is built to be a thoughtful conversationalist rather than a productivity tool. Free; no subscription tier as of writing.

## Setup

1. Go to [pi.ai](https://pi.ai), or download the iOS / Android app.
2. Sign in with Google, Apple, or phone number.
3. Start chatting. Pi greets you and adapts to the rhythm you set.
4. (Optional) Pick a voice (Pi has six TTS voices); voice mode is a standout feature.
5. (Optional) Connect WhatsApp or Messenger for chat over SMS style channels.

## How I use it day to day

I don't use Pi regularly; when I have:

* **For talking through something.** Pi is built to ask follow up questions and reflect back, not to optimize for solutions. Different feel from ChatGPT.
* **Voice mode while walking.** The natural sounding voices make voice conversations more pleasant than the equivalents on Claude or ChatGPT.
* **As a contrast.** When I want to feel the difference between "assistant tuned for utility" and "assistant tuned for presence," Pi is the clearest example.

It's not the model I'd use for code or research; Pi knows that and doesn't pretend.

## Gotchas

* Inflection's pivot in 2024 (founders to Microsoft) reduced the product's resourcing. Future development pace is uncertain.
* Pi declines a lot of utility tasks; it's tuned for conversation, not commands.
* Voice quality is excellent but token throughput in voice mode is bounded by latency; some pauses are noticeable.
* No paid tier means no SLA; treat it as a casual companion, not infrastructure.
