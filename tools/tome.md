# Tome

Tome started as an AI presentation tool (similar to Gamma) and pivoted in 2024 toward "AI sales narratives" — decks targeted at the sales motion, with the buyer persona and account context informing the AI's choices. In 2026 the product covers presentations broadly but the strongest use case remains sales heavy decks for B2B teams.

## What it actually is

A web based AI deck builder. Generate decks from a prompt or pasted source; AI handles structure, visuals, and content. Distinctive features: persona aware narratives (Tome asks who you're selling to and adjusts), tight Salesforce / HubSpot integration, embedded video and live data, mobile responsive output.

## Setup

1. Go to [tome.app](https://tome.app), sign up.
2. Free tier: 500 AI credits/mo.
3. Pricing: Pro $16/mo (unlimited AI), Team $26/seat/mo, Enterprise.
4. Click Create. Choose Generate (AI from prompt) or Upload (turn doc into deck).
5. (Sales focus) Connect your CRM; Tome can pull account context for personalised decks.

## How I use it day to day

* **Honest:** I've used Tome alongside Gamma; both are credible. My default is Gamma for general decks; Tome would be the choice if I were running B2B sales.
* **Sales decks personalised to account.** Tome pulls account data (industry, size, named contacts) and shapes the narrative. Cuts the "rebuild every deck" effort meaningfully.
* **Embedded interactive elements.** Live video, embedded webpages, calculators. Decks behave more like web pages than slides.
* **Mobile responsive output.** Decks render well on phones. Useful when prospects open links on the go.
* **Prompt to deck.** "Create a 10 slide pitch for an enterprise procurement team about workflow automation." Output is decent first draft; saves an hour vs scratch.

## Gotchas

* Pivoting toward sales has shifted some general purpose features. If you're not in sales, evaluate vs Gamma for the broader use case.
* Tier gating is real. Free tier is enough to evaluate; daily use needs Pro at minimum.
* Generated visuals are decent; not premium. For high stakes brand decks, replace AI imagery.
* Export to PowerPoint loses some interactive features (live embeds especially). Plan for this.
* The product changed name and positioning multiple times; check current docs / pricing rather than older posts.
